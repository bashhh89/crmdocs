# Email to CRM

Email to CRM reads inbound proposal-related emails, extracts key dates and details, matches them to the right opportunity, and applies verified updates to the CRM.

Open it at [proposals.anc.com/admin/email-to-crm](https://proposals.anc.com/admin/email-to-crm).

## Access

Email to CRM is available to Admins, Product Experts, and platform owners.

## When to use it

- You need to review inbound proposal emails before CRM updates are applied.
- An email mentions a proposal due date, submission requirement, or project detail.
- The system found an ambiguous opportunity match.
- You want to confirm what was written to the CRM and why.

## What it does

- Reads proposal mailbox intake.
- Extracts due dates and key details.
- Requires verbatim evidence before writing dates.
- Matches emails to opportunities.
- Automatically applies decisive matches.
- Holds ambiguous matches for review.
- Adds timeline notes for traceability.

## Typical workflow

1. Open [Email to CRM](https://proposals.anc.com/admin/email-to-crm).
2. Review queued email intakes.
3. Check the extracted date, quote evidence, and proposed opportunity match.
4. Approve, correct, or reject the update.
5. Confirm the CRM opportunity has the right due date and timeline note.

## Review checklist

- Due date has a verbatim source quote.
- Opportunity match is correct.
- Ambiguous matches are not auto-applied.
- Attachments and timeline notes are tied to the right account/opportunity.
