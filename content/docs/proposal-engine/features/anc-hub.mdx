## Updated navigation

Pages now have their own searchable library. The Hub keeps one short row for Resources, Pages, Timeline, Slack and Activity. **Find anything**, or Command K / Control K, searches the pages, resources and tools available to you.

The sidebar groups tools into **Intake**, **Build & estimate** and **Operations**. Open a group to find its tools; the current tool's group opens automatically. On your phone, use the menu button or search in the top bar.

## Find useful resources

Search the library or select a category. Use a bookmark to keep a resource in **Saved**. **Open resource** opens its destination; the small details arrow expands its summary, category and audience. Admins can edit or archive the card there.

**Add resource** accepts a link or description. The assistant prepares the details for review. Choose a cover palette, regenerate the cover, or upload an image. Review the destination and audience, then save. **More details** contains the summary, category and placement settings.

## Work with the assistant

Open **Assistant** to create a page or research a question beside your workspace. Choose a suggested prompt or browse **Prompt library**. **Improve** refines your wording before sending; **Undo** restores it. Watch the progress stages while a request runs and use **Stop** when needed.

**History** searches and reopens your saved chats. **Back** returns from history to the current chat. **New chat** starts a separate conversation. **Close** or Escape hides the panel without deleting your conversation. Closing the panel does not cancel a request already running.

For sourced answers, use **Research** in the assistant or **Explore with AI**. Check the linked sources before using the answer. **Save to library** opens a source in the resource form for review.

## Create and edit pages

Choose **Pages** to browse full titles, search the library, and filter published pages. Admins can also filter drafts, browse archives, restore a page, start a **Blank page**, or **Create with AI**. Open a page row and use **All pages** to return. The app warns before discarding unsaved edits when you leave a page.

A completed page request returns a real draft with **Open page** and **Edit & manage access**. In the editor, change the title and introduction, then use `/` to insert blocks. Use `@` for a teammate reference or an available app widget.

Select text and choose **Edit with AI** to rewrite that part using the page's allowed context. Review the suggestion before replacing the selection or inserting below. Use **Preview**, **Save draft** and **Publish page** as separate steps.

Set **Page audience** before sharing. A standalone page link follows the same access rules. Mentioning a person does not grant them access. App widgets also keep the destination app's own permissions. Ahmad's private usage reports cannot be shared by changing the page audience.

## Use the team spaces

**Timeline** supports updates, questions and wins, with resource attachments, likes and comments. Admins can pin and moderate conversations.

**Slack** shows the channels available to the signed-in person's verified Slack identity. Search and select a channel, then use **Open in Slack** to continue there. A Hub admin role does not bypass Slack membership.

**Activity** is separate from the conversation feed. Authorized users can filter recorded actions by person, app and period. Check each entry's identity, time and source, and read **Source coverage & limits**. An absent event does not prove that someone was inactive.

## Owner and admin controls

**Team & access** includes Hub tabs, Sidebar tabs, App widgets, AI access and Team roles. Owners manage roles; admins curate the workspace within their permissions. Ahmad can grant other admins specific assistant capabilities and sources. Some capabilities remain Ahmad only.

**View as user** is an Ahmad-only control. It enters the target account's real Hub experience. The banner identifies the active account and provides **Return to my account**. Changes are real and remain audited under Ahmad. The session expires, and linked apps keep their own sign-in rules.
