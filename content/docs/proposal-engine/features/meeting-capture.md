# Meeting Capture

Meeting Capture is the proposal-team intake point for call notes, recorder outputs, and meeting summaries. It helps keep client requirements from getting lost after calls.

Open it at [proposals.anc.com/meeting-capture](https://proposals.anc.com/meeting-capture).

## Access

Meeting Capture is available to Admins, Estimators, Proposal Leads, and platform owners.

## When to use it

- A client call includes scope, pricing, schedule, or follow-up requirements.
- Meeting notes come from Read.ai, Otter.ai, Recall.ai, desktop recording, or manual notes.
- You need a meeting summary tied back to proposal work.
- You want action items captured for follow-up.

## What it can capture

- Meeting title and source.
- Recording or transcript references.
- Summary notes.
- Action items.
- Client requirements and open questions.
- Links back to CRM or proposal work where available.

## Typical workflow

1. Open [Meeting Capture](https://proposals.anc.com/meeting-capture).
2. Add the meeting source or notes.
3. Review extracted summary and action items.
4. Tie the information back to the right proposal, opportunity, or follow-up workflow.
5. Use the captured requirements in Estimator, SOW Builder, or CRM follow-up.

## Review checklist

- Meeting source is correct.
- The summary captures decisions, not just transcript text.
- Follow-up owners and dates are clear.
- Proposal-impacting requirements are moved into the right project or estimate.
