[Open ANC Studio](https://classroom.basheer.app)

Choose **Propose** above the familiar brief box. Describe the client presentation you need, attach the source documents and select **Build the pitch**. The existing creation sequence ends in a client presentation, with the estimate available beside it.

## Start with the source

Use the paperclip to attach an RFP, display schedule or pricing workbook. Attach up to five PDF or Excel files, with a combined size of 50 MB. Use one authoritative pricing workbook per proposal. You can also start with a written brief containing the client, project and display specifications.

An RFP or brief uses ANC estimating rates. A valid ANC pricing workbook with a Margin Analysis sheet preserves its selling prices.

## Review any flagged details

When the source has missing specifications or review notes, creation pauses at **A few details before we build**. Check client and project names, display dimensions in feet, pitch in millimetres, quantities and installation environment. Review installation complexity, union labor and bond requirements.

Read the source citations and warnings, make any corrections and select **Continue creating**. Additional equipment or services may need separate pricing. A complete source without flagged details can continue automatically.

## Present and download

The proposal opens on the original canvas with client presentation pages. Use the page arrows to review it or **Play** to present with narration. The investment follows the completed estimate. Review every page before sharing.

Open **Estimate** beside the presentation to inspect the actual ANC PDF. Download **Estimate PDF** for the client package and **Internal workbook** for the cost review.

Generated prices are budget estimates subject to site verification, final design, and confirmed supplier and subcontractor pricing. Review the internal workbook before committing a client price.

## Share the estimate

In **Estimate**, choose **Create client link**, then copy or open the link. It shares the completed PDF only. Internal workbooks and source files remain private. Use **Revoke** to stop access.

The pitch and its estimate stay together in the saved presentation.
