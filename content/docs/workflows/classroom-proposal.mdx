[Open ANC Studio Proposal](https://classroom.basheer.app/proposal)

Proposal creates a branded estimate package. Start with the source documents, review the scope and pricing, then choose the PDF version to send to the client.

## Start with the requirements

Open **Propose** in ANC Studio. Choose files or drop an RFP, display schedule or pricing workbook into the Proposal workspace. You can attach up to five PDF or Excel files, with a combined size of 50 MB. Use one authoritative pricing workbook per proposal.

Add the client and project names and any useful instructions in **Project brief or instructions**. You can also start with a written brief alone. Select **Build proposal package**.

An RFP or brief uses ANC estimating rates. A valid ANC pricing workbook with a Margin Analysis sheet preserves its selling prices. The source documents stay with the saved proposal.

## Review the display scope

Open **Scope & pricing**. Check the client, project, venue and location, then review each display's dimensions in feet, pixel pitch in millimetres, quantity and installation environment.

Read the source citations and review notes. Missing specifications remain highlighted for correction. The estimate cannot generate until the required details are complete. Additional equipment and services identified in an RFP may need separate pricing; review those requirements before committing the price.

For an estimate built from requirements, review the installation setting, union labor and bond requirement. Choose **Generate estimate** after completing the review. A complete brief without flagged details can produce the initial estimate automatically.

## Check and download the package

**Estimate & delivery** shows the total and the ANC PDF. Use the page arrows to inspect the pricing and specifications, then select **Estimate PDF** to download it.

Use **Download cost workbook** for the internal estimate. Review that workbook before committing a client price. Generated prices are budget estimates subject to site verification, final design, and confirmed supplier and subcontractor pricing.

To revise a saved estimate, edit **Scope & pricing** and rebuild it. The version selector keeps previous completed PDFs available. For a supplied pricing workbook, change the original workbook and start a new proposal to use its updated prices.

## Share one completed version

Select the completed PDF version you want, then choose **Create client link**. Copy the link or open it to check the client view.

The client link contains that PDF only. Internal cost workbooks and source documents remain private. Rebuilding the proposal does not change an existing link's PDF. Use **Revoke** to stop access to a link.

The client sees ANC branding and the estimate, with no classroom or teacher workflow.
