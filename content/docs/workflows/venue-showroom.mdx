## Set the scene

Choose **Venue** in the bottom toolbar. Select the venue type and attendance. The audience-view buttons give you spectator, ground-level, suite, broadcast, and overview positions. **Event lights** and **Daylight** change the lighting. **Take a tour** moves through the views; **Pause tour** stops it.

The supplied models are illustrative planning environments, not surveyed replicas of named venues. For an authored venue model or the existing operational tools, choose **Open advanced workspace** in the Venue panel.

## Choose the screens

Choose **Screens** to enable or disable individual displays. Click a board directly in the venue to open its Content panel. **Screen settings** shows the selected display’s dimensions and product.

## Put content on the displays

In **Content**, use **Play on** to choose **All active screens** or the selected display.

- **ANC library** provides a 24-second venue showcase, the official ANC Studios football reel, a blue motion takeover, and a static ANC signature. Select a creative to apply it immediately.
- **Video** accepts MP4 or WebM files. Videos loop automatically.
- **Logo / image** accepts PNG, JPG, or WebP. Transparent logos display against a black screen face.

Files must be under 100 MB. **Fit** keeps the whole image or video visible without distortion. **Fill** crops the content to cover the display. Check the result from each audience angle.

## Control playback

The **Screen content** player stays visible as you move around the venue. Use **Pause / Play**, restart, the timeline, or **Sound off / Sound on**. Matching screens share the same video playback. ANC motion graphics also follow the timeline. Camera tour controls are separate from content playback.

ANC library selections remain in saved and shared designs. Uploaded files remain in the current browser session and need to be added again in another session.

## Review and present

Choose **Present** to review the venue, active displays, and number of screens with custom content. **Enter presentation** removes the editing panels. Use **Next view** or **Play tour** to guide the room through the design.

**Before / after** temporarily turns displays off without changing the configuration. **Show design** restores the configured display state. Use **Exit presentation** or Escape to return to editing.

**Save design** stores screen settings in this browser. **Copy share link** creates a configuration link. Add local uploads again when opening the design in another session. Package values, when expanded, are scenario estimates from preset rates and assigned sponsors; they are not quotations or verified venue revenue.

## Before sharing

- Check the content from the audience positions that matter to the client.
- Confirm which displays are enabled.
- Check whether any local files need to be supplied separately.
- Review the illustrative venue and commercial assumptions.
