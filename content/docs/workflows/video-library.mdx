## Find a video without remembering its name

Use the search field at the top of the library. Enter a topic, a workflow, or an idea, such as **who is covering the venue today**, **stadium LED screens**, or **build a presentation for a client**.

Search matches meaning, topics, and titles. Use the length filter or change the sort order to narrow the results. **All videos** shows the complete catalog, and the category links in the left sidebar let you browse one kind of video at a time. On a phone, open the menu to find the same navigation.

## Watch and return to your place

Select a video to open the player. Use the standard controls to play, pause, seek, adjust volume, or go full screen. The controls below the screen let you change playback speed, expand into **theater mode**, or use picture in picture when your browser supports it.

The running order beside the player shows what comes next. Turn **Autoplay** off if you want playback to stop after the current video. **Continue watching** returns you to videos you have started.

## Keep a shortlist or create a playlist

Use a video’s bookmark button to add it to **Saved videos**. Select it again to remove the bookmark.

To collect several videos, use the three-dot button on a video card or **Playlist** below the player. Choose an existing playlist, or enter a name and select **Create playlist**. Open **My playlists** to play, rename, reorder, or remove your selections.

Your saved videos, playlists, queue, and playback position stay with this browser. They do not follow you to a different browser or device, and clearing this site’s cookies starts a new personal library.

## Set a running order

**Collections** provides curated viewing paths, including **The ANC story**, **Run the venue**, and **Pitch & present**. Open a collection and select **Play collection** to start it, or **Queue all** to add it to your running order.

Use the **+** button on an individual video card, or **Queue** below the player, to add one video. Open the queue button at the top right to move videos earlier or later, remove a selection, or play the queue.

## Share the exact moment

Select **Share** below the player. Keep **Start at** checked to send the current playback position, or uncheck it to start from the beginning. Choose **Copy link** and send that link to your colleague.

Shared links include viewing attribution. **Viewing analytics** at the bottom of the sidebar opens the reporting area for viewers with analytics access. **Download** saves the video file itself.
