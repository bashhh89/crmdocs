## Start with the promise and proof

The opening screen makes one claim: **One partner. Every screen. Every moment.** Directly below it, three sourced proof points connect that promise to work at Levi's Stadium, Fenway Park, and Xfinity Mobile Arena.

Use **See the proof** to move straight into the project work. Use **Start a project** when a buyer is ready to define what ANC should solve.

## Follow the buyer's reason for visiting

The page offers three routes instead of asking every visitor to understand ANC's organization first:

- **Build or transform a venue** leads to design, integration, and the connected display system.
- **Run the experience every event** leads to operations, service, and game-day continuity.
- **Put a brand inside the moment** leads to media, sponsorship, and measurable fan attention.

Each route lands in the relevant capability section. The complete navigation remains available for visitors who want to browse work, capabilities, experience, and people.

## See the connected experience

Scroll through the cinematic venue sequence to see how ANC's disciplines work together: the display canvas, control, content, sponsorship, and live support. Reduced-motion settings preserve the story without forcing animation.

## Open the work behind the claim

The project grid contains sourced ANC work rather than generic marketing tiles. Open a project to see the venue, ANC's role, the available evidence, and related capabilities. Filters help a buyer narrow the proof by market or need.

The wider site includes 13 project stories, six capability areas, ANC people, partner context, company news, and source notes.

## Turn interest into a project brief

Select **Start a project** from the opening screen or navigation. Choose the buyer's goal, venue type, and timing. The site turns those choices into a concise brief and carries the buyer to ANC's contact path.

The brief is deliberately short. It captures enough context to begin a useful conversation without forcing a long form before the buyer understands the value.
