## Estimate: state the assumptions

Describe the dimensions, pitch, environment, mounting and service access. State the pricing assumptions rather than letting them be guessed. In this training example, a 16 × 9 ft indoor wall uses 4 mm pitch, 35% margin, 5% contingency and 7% tax.

The workbook follows the estimator's convention: contingency on direct cost, tax on cost plus contingency, then margin. Its yellow input cells remain editable. These reference rates and assumptions are not an approved customer quote.

## Proposal: carry the same figures forward

Continue the same conversation and ask for a budget proposal using the estimate. Check the final PDF against the workbook, including cents. The example contains scope, specifications, pricing and exclusions. It contains no signature request or acceptance authorization.

## Design: review the actual boards

Supply the exact copy, colors, treatment and dimensions. The demo requests a static 1280 × 720 main board and a static 3840 × 240 ribbon. Proof images include their review surround and may be scaled; their PNG dimensions are not the native delivery dimensions.

Inspect the whole headline, subline and layout before approval. Paid final rendering is a separate action, with its cost and approval requirements preserved.

## CRM: verify the saved change

The first request creates one unlinked training note. The follow-up changes that same note's title to **ANC Academy Demo — ready for review** and adds **Estimate and proposal checked.**

The walkthrough opens the CRM record itself and the exported PDF. Other records are not changed and no notification is sent.

## Service operations: distinguish counts from conclusions

Ask for a read-only snapshot when you need context, not operational changes. The example includes venues, events, assignments, tickets and maintenance, with a definition beside each count.

An assignment count is not a headcount. An unassigned event count does not establish the staffing requirement. A maintenance count is not a count of unique venues or confirmed equipment failures.

## A useful way to ask

> Prepare the finished output for this task using the connected ANC system. Keep the supplied scope and figures unchanged, state any missing assumptions, and verify the result. Do not send, approve or change unrelated records.
