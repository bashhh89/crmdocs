## Start with the work that needs you

In the Proposal Engine sidebar, open **Bid Intake**. The queue groups related messages into the same pursuit.

- **Needs review** puts outstanding decisions in front of you.
- **Due soon** shows verified bid deadlines within the next 14 days.
- **Read failures** brings together messages that need to be read again.
- **Completed** shows emails already dealt with.
- **All mail** includes the remaining correspondence.

Search by venue, project, or email. Select a queue row to open its details beside the list. On a phone, the details fill the screen; **Back to queue** returns you to the same row.

## Check the evidence before deciding

Read the bid deadline and **Next action** at the top of the selected email. In **Summary & dates**, each extracted date appears beside the sender's words and its evidence label. Open **Original email** to check the full message.

When a pursuit contains multiple emails, use the message selector to read the relevant message. Attachment names are references; open the source email to access the files.

## Choose the opportunity and review the dates

Select **Choose opportunity** beside the correct match. If the right opportunity is not listed, open **Not one of these? Assign to a different opportunity** and search for it.

The confirmation shows the selected opportunity and its current proposal deadline when available. Review **Also apply verified bid and RFI deadlines from this email** carefully. Leave it unchecked to preserve the CRM's current deadlines.

**Confirm & apply** adds the email summary to the opportunity's timeline and applies the chosen date option. **Cancel** returns to review without applying the choice. If no opportunity exists, review **Draft new opportunity** instead.

## Add an email yourself

Open **Process an email**. Add the subject and sender, then paste the full message into **Email message**, including the deadline wording. Use **Extract & match** to prepare it for review in the queue.

## Understand an alert outcome

Open **Bid Alerts** from the shared tabs. Choose the time window, search for the venue, or filter by **Announced**, **Held for review**, **Not announced**, or **Tests**.

Select an entry to read its outcome and reason. **Latest alert** opens the latest actual announcement in the loaded time window, excluding tests. **Review source email** returns to the corresponding intake email.

Reviewing an alert does not resend it. Bid Intake decisions update the CRM; the intake page does not send Slack alerts.
