[Open Inside the Work](https://inside.basheer.app)

## Choose your route

Select **Take the guided tour** to follow the six exhibits in order. **Next** and **Previous exhibit** move through the story. In guided mode, the left and right arrow keys also change exhibits. Escape returns to the stadium.

For independent exploration, select a screen or an exhibit in the side navigation. Drag the stadium to look around. On a phone, use **Exhibits** to open the navigation. **Chapter view** provides a reading view without the stadium.

The stadium is an illustrative exhibition environment. Its screens organize the work; they are not a surveyed venue layout.

## Start with Presentations

The first exhibit contains a published, ten-page example drawn from the ANC master. Explore the client view, open it in a separate window, and compare it with the example PDF. On a phone, select **Open the presentation** beneath the preview.

**How the workflow fits** explains the path from reusable content to a client handoff. Opening the team presentation workspace requires team sign-in.

## Compare the work

- **Creative proof:** play accepted creative beside a new comparison concept. Check the message, distance readability, and motion. The labels identify the approval status.
- **Every screen:** select the main board, half tower, wide ribbon, or compact panel. Each plays an actual accepted file at its original aspect ratio.
- **Venue experiences:** compare a source photograph with sponsor and concert concepts, or play ANC's venue reel. Generated concepts are labeled for discussion.
- **Design workflow:** follow a real Lumen Field request through the brief, design, review, and delivery. The review step connects the designer, recorded decision, and accepted files.

## Keep the next discussion focused

In **Next together**, review the practical checklist and save observations against an exhibit. Select **Download observations** to keep a text copy for the team. Observations and checked items are saved only in the current browser; they are not sent to anyone automatically.

**Share** copies a link to the current exhibit. Anyone opening it can continue the review independently. The printable [field guide](https://inside.basheer.app/field-guide.html) keeps the six stops together.

## Present in a meeting

If you have been given a private presenter link, open it on your own screen and select **Open audience window**. Share the audience window in the meeting. Use the presenter’s chapter controls to move the shared view while keeping speaking notes and discussion cues on your screen.

Share the main stadium link with reviewers afterward. Keep the private presenter link for the person running the meeting.
