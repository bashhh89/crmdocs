## Two apps inside the CRM — pick the right one

When you sign into the CRM, the top-left says either **ANC** or **Standard**. They look similar but show different things:

- **ANC app** — what 99% of users want. Has the ANC dashboards, custom views, the Scout AI helper, and folders for Proposals, Venues, Inventory, Design, Advertising, and Products.
- **Standard app** — vanilla layout. Useful only for the platform default views.

If you land on Standard, click the app switcher (top-left) and pick **ANC**. The CRM remembers your choice.

## Daily / weekly / monthly rhythm

Different teams check different things at different beats:

- **Daily**:
  - Natalia: Estimation & Proposals view → what's due today
  - Tech support leads: Service Dashboard → open tickets, anything missing its deadline
  - Operations managers: Service Dashboard → tonight's events, staffing gaps
- **Weekly** (Monday):
  - Sales: Proposal Pipeline kanban → what's moving
  - Finance: Backlog view → won deals + payment percentage unpaid
- **Monthly / quarterly**:
  - Jireh: ANC 2026 Dashboard → revenue and margin by vertical
  - Leadership: Win/Loss by League → RFP performance trend

## When something feels off

| Symptom | What to check |
|---|---|
| "I sent a proposal but it's not in the CRM" | Open the deal in the CRM. The proposal pushes within a few seconds. If it's not there after a minute, flag it. |
| "I can't see the ANC dashboards" | You're in the Standard app. Click the app switcher (top-left) and pick **ANC**. |
| "My ticket isn't in the CRM" | Should appear within a second. If still missing, it's likely a venue-name mismatch. |
| "The CRM shows stale revenue on a deal" | Open the deal, edit any year's revenue field, and save. The total recalculates. |
| "I can't log in to the CRM on my phone" | Has your account ever signed in with Microsoft? Use the public invite link to complete the first sign-in. |
