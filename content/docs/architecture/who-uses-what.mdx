## By app inside Twenty CRM

The CRM ships with two apps. Pick the right one:

- **ANC app** — the one Ahmad customized for the ANC team. Has the ANC dashboards, custom views, the Scout agent, and the folder structure (Proposals / Venues & Field / Inventory & Hardware / Design & Content / Advertising / Products & Pricing). **This is what 99% of users want.**
- **Standard app** — the vanilla Twenty layout. Useful only for the platform default views. If you land here on first login, click the app switcher (top-left) and pick **ANC**.

The CRM remembers which app you last used.

## By team's "moment of truth"

Different teams check different things at different beats. If you're staffing the org around the tools, this is the rhythm:

- **Daily** (every morning):
  - Natalia: Estimation & Proposals view → what's due today
  - Tech support leads: Service Dashboard → open tickets, SLA at risk
  - Operations managers: Service Dashboard → tonight's events, staffing gaps
- **Weekly** (Monday):
  - Sales: Proposal Pipeline kanban → what's moving
  - Finance: Backlog view → won deals + payment % unpaid
- **Monthly** / quarterly:
  - Jireh: ANC 2026 Dashboard → revenue + margin by vertical
  - Leadership: Win/Loss by League → RFP performance trend

## When something feels off

| Symptom | Where to look first |
|---|---|
| "I sent a proposal but it's not in the CRM" | Proposal Engine — every save pushes to CRM. Open the Opportunity in CRM to confirm. If missing >30s after save, flag to engineering. |
| "I can't see ANC dashboards" | You're in the Standard app. Switch to ANC. |
| "My ticket isn't in the CRM" | Service Dashboard ticket sync runs ~1s. If absent after a minute, see [Service Dashboard troubleshooting](/docs/service-dashboard/how-tos). |
| "The CRM shows stale revenue" | The per-FY rollup runs on opportunity update. If a deal looks stale, edit and save the FY field — rollup refires. |
| "I can't log in to the CRM on my phone" | Has your account ever logged in via Microsoft SSO? Some accounts were provisioned but never completed the handshake. Use the public invite link to onboard. |
