## What the empty objects mean

A handful of objects have zero records today. Most of them are **infrastructure built in advance**, ready to populate as the next phase of work lands:

- **Ad Campaigns / Ad Channels / Ad Deliverables** — the advertising / sponsorship side. Schema ready; data flowing in starts when sponsorship reporting lights up.
- **Estimates / Estimate Lines** — wired for Scout's Quick Estimate skill. Each one Scout produces creates rows here.
- **Manufacturers / Rate Cards** — upstream of LED Products; the products carry the data today.
- **Print Requests / RMA Trackers / Parts** — operational pipelines staged for when the warehouse system connects.
- **RFP Analyses** — the Proposal Engine analyses live there today; the link into the CRM is queued for the next phase.

These aren't dead weight — they're scaffolding. As each upgrade in the [What Else Is Possible](/docs/whats-possible) section lands, more of these zeroes turn into live numbers.

## How the numbers grew

For comparison: at the start of April 2026 the CRM had **8,360 deals**. Today it has **9,994** — about **1,600 new deals** added in three weeks of normal sales operation. The CRM is keeping up with the actual rate of business.

## What this proves for the meeting

- **Real scale**: 20,090 people, 150,000 events, 28,000 time entries — this is not a sandbox.
- **Account-centric**: every record above hangs off a Company (or rolls up to one). One screen, one client.
- **Live and current**: the deal count grew by nearly 20% in three weeks.
- **Built for the future**: 14+ scaffolded objects are ready for the next features without schema work.
