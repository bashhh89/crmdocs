# How the Three ANC Tools Fit Together

ANC runs on three connected tools. Each one is built for a specific part of the business. They share information automatically — nothing has to be retyped between them.

| Tool | URL | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Proposal Engine** | [proposals.anc.com](https://proposals.anc.com) | Pricing deals, generating proposals, building SOWs, responding to RFPs |
| **CRM** | [crm.ancsports.net](https://crm.ancsports.net) | The single record of every account, deal, dashboard, and report |
| **Service Dashboard** | [services.ancsports.net](https://services.ancsports.net) | Day-to-day operations — events, tickets, technicians, venue health, IoT operations |

The CRM sits in the middle. It's the **single source of truth**. The other two tools are where the work actually happens — the Proposal Engine for new business, the Service Dashboard for running existing business. Both push their state into the CRM continuously, so leadership always sees one truth.

## What each tool answers

| Question | Where to look |
|---|---|
| What's our pipeline? What's the forecast? | **CRM** — Pipeline by Business Unit, Revenue Forecast, ANC 2026 Dashboard |
| What's due to a client this week? | **CRM** — Estimation & Proposals view (Natalia's daily) |
| How do I price this RFP? | **Proposal Engine** — Estimator + RFP Analyzer |
| What are we sending the client tomorrow? | **Proposal Engine** — proposal PDFs, SOWs, submittals |
| Who's working tonight's game? | **Service Dashboard** — Events + Staff Assignments |
| Is this venue having issues? | **Service Dashboard** — Venue page, ticket status, IoT Operations Workspace |
| Did the install team finish post-game? | **Service Dashboard** — Workflow status |
| What displays, devices, rack notes, and walkthrough records exist at a venue? | **Service Dashboard** — IoT Operations Workspace |
| What's every artifact ever sent to this client? | **CRM** — open the Company → all the tabs |

## How information moves between them

Three flows run automatically. Nobody has to copy anything from one tool to another.

### 1. Proposal Engine → CRM

Every time someone generates a proposal, an Excel cost sheet, a SOW, or a one-pager, it appears in the CRM under the right deal — with a timeline note showing who made it and when.

Sales never has to "also update the CRM."

### 2. Service Dashboard → CRM

Every ticket, event, and game-day step in the Service Dashboard appears in the CRM within about a second.

Joe's operations team keeps using the Service Dashboard as their daily home. The CRM gets the operational truth without forcing them to switch tools.

### 3. CRM → AI helpers

The CRM has a built-in AI helper called **Scout**. Scout can answer questions about the pipeline, draft proposals, generate venue mockup images, or pull together a venue health report. The same brain is also available in Slack as **@ANC** — anything you can ask Scout in the CRM, you can ask @ANC in Slack.
