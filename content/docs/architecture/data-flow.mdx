# Data Flow Between the Three Tools

When you do something in one tool, here's what shows up automatically in the others. Nothing in this list requires a person to copy or upload anything by hand.

## Quick overview

| When you do this... | ...this shows up here | ...this fast |
|---|---|---|
| Generate a proposal PDF in the Proposal Engine | The PDF + a timeline note appear on the deal in the CRM | A few seconds |
| Generate a SOW in the Proposal Engine | The SOW file + a timeline note appear on the deal in the CRM | A few seconds |
| Export an Excel cost sheet | The Excel file appears on the deal in the CRM | A few seconds |
| Generate a one-pager (Mirror Mode) | The deal in the CRM is automatically marked "Pricing Complete" | Same moment |
| Create a ticket in the Service Dashboard | The ticket appears on the venue's Company page in the CRM | About 1 second |
| Update a ticket (status, priority, assignment) | The CRM shows the latest version | About 1 second |
| Schedule a new event | The event appears on the venue and account in the CRM | About 1 second |
| Technician checks in / files game-ready / submits post-game | The CRM shows the live workflow status | About 1 second |
| Add a new product to the Proposal Engine catalog | The product appears in the CRM's product list | A few seconds |

## Proposal Engine → CRM

Everything the Proposal Engine produces ends up on the matching deal in the CRM, automatically.

### What you'll see in the CRM after each action

- **New proposal saved** — if the company didn't exist yet, it gets created. The deal is created and linked. You'll see the deal under the Company.
- **Proposal PDF generated** — the PDF is attached to the deal, and a note in the timeline says who made it and when. The deal's bid status moves to "Bid Submitted."
- **SOW generated (Premium or Installation)** — the Word doc is attached, with a timeline note.
- **Excel cost sheet exported** — the spreadsheet is attached, with a timeline note.
- **One-pager generated (Mirror Mode)** — the deal is automatically marked "Pricing Complete" on the date you generated it.

### Why this matters

Before this, sales had a two-step habit: generate the file in the Proposal Engine, then upload it manually to the CRM. About 4 in 10 times, the second step never happened — so proposals went out without ever appearing on the right Account. Now every artifact lands in the right place automatically.

## Service Dashboard → CRM

Every operational change in the Service Dashboard shows up in the CRM in about a second. Joe's team keeps working in the Service Dashboard. Leadership sees the real-time picture in the CRM.

### What you'll see in the CRM

- **A new ticket** — appears on the Company's "Tickets" tab. If the venue is linked to an active won deal, the ticket also shows up on that deal.
- **A ticket update** — status, priority, assignment, or resolution all sync within a second.
- **A new event** (game, concert, service event) — appears on the Company's "Events" tab and on the linked deal.
- **An event update** — date changes, league, summary, all sync.
- **Workflow status** — when a tech checks in, files game-ready, or submits the post-game report, the CRM reflects it live.

### When the CRM is briefly unavailable

The Service Dashboard never blocks. Tickets still get created, technicians still finish their workflow, events still save. The CRM just briefly shows the older version, and a safety net catches it within a few minutes.

## How leadership sees one combined picture

Because both other tools push into the CRM, leadership only needs to open one tool to see the whole picture:

- All sold work — by Natalia and the sales team — visible on the Opportunity record
- All operational work — by Joe's team — visible as Tickets and Events on the Company record
- All revenue and forecast — visible on the dashboards

This is what Jireh asked for: an account-centric view where you open one Company and see everything happening with that client.

## Salesforce (read-only)

Salesforce was the previous CRM. ANC has read-only access during the transition window so anything that comes up about historical data can be cross-checked. No data flows from the new CRM back to Salesforce — it's purely a reference.

## What does NOT yet flow automatically

These are gaps to be aware of — they're on the list to add later:

- **Email → deal** — replying to a client email today doesn't automatically create or update a deal in the CRM. Manual.
- **Service ticket → deal auto-link** — when a ticket comes in for a venue with an active won deal, the link to that specific deal isn't filled in automatically yet. The ticket still shows up on the Company page; just not pre-attached to the right deal.
- **Accounting** — payment data isn't yet pulled into the CRM forecasting widgets.

Each of these is on the explicit roadmap with a sizing estimate.
