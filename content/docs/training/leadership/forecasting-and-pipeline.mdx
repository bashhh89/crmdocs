**After this lesson** you'll understand how the forecast is calculated, when to trust it, and how to focus on the realistic pipeline.


## How the forecast works

The forecast rolls up **expected revenue across the live opportunities**, weighted by where deals stand. Because it's tied to live deal data, it stays current as deals move — it's not a stale snapshot someone exported last month.

**Or just ask:** *"What's the forecast for Technology this year?"*

<Callout title="Can you trust it?">
It's as current as the team's last update. It reflects the real pipeline rather than a guess — so the honest rule is: keep the deals current and the forecast is honest. Garbage-in still applies, which is exactly why stage/status discipline matters across the team.
</Callout>

## Filtering to likely deals

You can filter by **probability** — for example to **50% and above** — so you're looking at the realistic pipeline rather than every long shot. That mirrors how the team has always weighted the forecast.

**Or just ask:** *"Show me the pipeline at 50% probability and up."*

## Comparing periods

The dashboards let you look across periods, so you see the **trend**, not just a single snapshot.

**Or just ask:** *"Compare this quarter to last."*

## Now you try →

Open the forecast, filter the pipeline to 50%+ probability, and compare it to the unfiltered number — that gap is your long-shot exposure.
