**After this lesson** you'll be able to build your own at-a-glance picture, get numbers out for a board deck, and pull any figure by just asking.


## Pin what you care about

Pin the views and KPIs you check often so they're **one click away**. You build your own at-a-glance picture instead of hunting for the same reports each time.

## Board-ready views

The dashboards give clean roll-ups — pipeline, won, forecast, win/loss by league, by vertical — that work for a leadership or board conversation. Pin the ones you present from so they're ready when you need them.

## Exporting and sharing outside the system

When you need to hand numbers to someone outside the CRM:

- **Export a view to a spreadsheet** — the data comes out clean in a format people already use.
- Or pull the figure via the assistant and drop it straight into an email or deck.

**Or just ask:** *"Export this view to Excel."*

## Just ask for the number

Often the fastest path is no report at all — just ask:

- *"What have we won this quarter?"*
- *"What's the forecast for Technology this year?"*
- *"Compare pipeline this quarter to last."*
- *"Which sponsors are behind their contracted games?"*

The assistant pulls the answer directly from the live data — no navigating required.

<Callout title="How current is all of this?">
Live. As the team updates opportunities, the dashboards and forecasts move with them — so what you're looking at reflects where things stand right now, not last month's export.
</Callout>
