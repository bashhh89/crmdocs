## Win/Loss by league

There's a **win-and-loss view by league**, so you can see win rates and patterns across MLB, NBA, NFL, NCAA, and the rest — useful both for spotting where you're strongest and for case-study pulls.

**Or just ask:** *"What's our RFP win rate by league this year?"*

## Why it stays accurate

This only works if deals are marked **won / lost / no-bid** as they resolve — which is the habit the vertical teams learn in their tracks. When that's kept current, the win/loss picture is real, not guesswork.

## Pulling a reference

Filter won deals by league or type to find comparable past wins — handy when you need a reference or a case study for a new pitch.

**Or just ask:** *"Show me our won NBA deals over $1M."*

## Now you try →

Open the win/loss view, pick a league, and read the win rate — then ask the assistant for the won deals behind it.
