## The big picture

The dashboards roll everything up — **pipeline, won business, forecast** — and they update as the team works, so the picture is always current.

**Or just ask:** *"What's our pipeline versus won this quarter?"*

## By vertical

The data is organized by the **three sides of the business** — Technology, Venue Services, and Media & Sponsorship. Pick a vertical's tab to narrow everything to that side, or see the combined total. It mirrors how the business is actually structured.

**Or just ask:** *"Show me the Technology numbers."* · *"Compare revenue and margin by vertical."*

## Biggest and at-risk deals

- **Biggest:** there's a top-open-deals view, and you can sort the pipeline by value.
- **At-risk:** filter to deals that have gone quiet or are past a due date — the ones that need a nudge before they slip.

**Or just ask:** *"What are our biggest open opportunities?"* · *"What's overdue or stalled?"*

Open the company dashboard, switch to one vertical's tab, then ask the assistant for your biggest open deal in that vertical.
