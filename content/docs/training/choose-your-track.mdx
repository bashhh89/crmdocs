### Q2: Are you trying to do work or read work?

- **Do work:** pick Sales / Technology, Services, Media & Sponsorship, or Proposal Engine.
- **Read work:** pick Leadership or dashboards.
- **Clean work:** pick Working Standard first.

### Q3: Is the answer client-facing?

- **Yes:** verify the source records before sending.
- **No:** use the assistant for speed, then clean the record if you find a gap.

## Real Scenarios

### Sales Scenario

It is Thursday. A team asks for an LED board budget before Monday. You need to know whether the Company already exists, what similar work is on file, whether pricing is complete, and what the next proposal step is.

Open:

1. [Core: Finding Anything Fast](/docs/training/core/finding-anything)
2. [Sales & Account Management](/docs/training/tracks/sales-crm)
3. [Technology: Your Pipeline](/docs/training/technology/pipeline)
4. [Proposal Engine: Estimator](/docs/proposal-engine/features/estimator)

<Callout title="Where this lives">
Company history and pipeline status live in the CRM. Pricing and proposal work live in the Proposal Engine. Finished proposal artifacts attach back to the CRM record.
</Callout>

### Services Scenario

It is game week. A venue has an open display issue, a scheduled event, and a client asking whether the site is ready. You need the Company, tickets, event activity, and recent service notes in one place.

Open:

1. [Core: Getting Around](/docs/training/core/orientation)
2. [Services & Operations](/docs/training/tracks/services)
3. [Venue Services: Service Tickets](/docs/training/venue-services/service-tickets)
4. [Service Dashboard: Tickets](/docs/service-dashboard/features/tickets)

<Callout title="Where this lives">
Client and Company context live in the CRM. Day-to-day service work lives in the Service Dashboard. Synced ticket and event history rolls back into the CRM Company view.
</Callout>

### Proposal Scenario

An RFP lands with a tight deadline. You need to triage the document, price the deal, generate the SOW, and make sure the final files are attached to the right CRM opportunity.

Open:

1. [Core: Working Standard](/docs/training/core/working-standard)
2. [Proposals & Estimation](/docs/training/tracks/proposals)
3. [Proposal Engine: RFP Analyzer](/docs/proposal-engine/features/rfp-analyzer)
4. [Proposal Engine: SOW Generation](/docs/proposal-engine/features/sow-generation)
5. [Proposal Engine: Universal CRM Push](/docs/proposal-engine/features/universal-crm-push)

<Callout title="Glossary">
<abbr title="Scope of Work: the document defining what ANC will deliver.">SOW</abbr>, <abbr title="Request For Proposal: the client's document asking ANC to bid on work.">RFP</abbr>, <abbr title="Exact-fidelity Excel-to-PDF proposal output.">Mirror Mode</abbr>, and <abbr title="Auto-attach behavior that places generated proposal files on the right CRM deal.">Universal CRM Push</abbr> are defined in the [glossary](/docs/glossary).
</Callout>
