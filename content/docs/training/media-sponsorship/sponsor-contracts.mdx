## What contracts hold

Sponsor contracts are tracked and tied to the **sponsor and team**, so you can see what was committed — a full season, a partial, or a minimum number of games — right alongside what's actually running.

**Or just ask:** *"How many games is Tire Rack contracted for with the Red Sox?"*

## Contracted vs. actual

This is the payoff. The **contract** records hold what was committed; the **placement** records hold what's actually running. Compare them on the sponsor's account to see the gap.

**Or just ask:** *"Show me contracted versus actual for Bigelow."*

## Spotting an under-delivering sponsor

Compare contracted games to what's actually run — the gap tells you who's behind, while there's still time to make it right.

**Or just ask:** *"Which sponsors are behind their contracted games?"*

## Adding a new sponsor

Add the sponsor as a **company**, then their placements and contracts tie to that account.

**Or just ask:** *"Add a new sponsor called [name]."*

## Now you try →

Pick a sponsor, open their account, and check their contracted count against what's actually run this season.
