## How placements work

Each placement is its **own record** — the game, the sponsor, the position — tied to the sponsor and the account. So you can see everything a sponsor has running *and* everything happening at a given property, without rebuilding it in a spreadsheet.

## Finding placements

There's a placements view you can slice any way you need:

- **By month:** filter to the month — *"show me this month's placements."*
- **By game or date:** *"what's running at tonight's game?"*
- **By sponsor:** *"show me everything Bigelow has running."*
- **By team:** filter to the property you're prepping for.

**Or just ask** the assistant any of the above in plain English.

## Logging a new placement

1. Create a **placement record**.
2. Set its **game, sponsor, and position**.
3. It links to the sponsor's account automatically.

**Or just ask:** *"Log a homeplate placement for Tire Rack at the Red Sox game on the 12th."*

## Marking what actually ran

Each placement has a **status** — scheduled, executed, not executed, and so on. Set it as things run. That status is what lets you tell what actually aired versus what was planned.

**Or just ask:** *"Mark the Bigelow placement on the 12th as executed."*

<Callout title="National vs. local">
The placement record captures the relevant TV/coverage detail, so national versus local is on the record — which lets you report and verify them correctly instead of lumping them together.
</Callout>

## It all ties together

Every placement links to the **sponsor's account** (so the account reflects what's running) and can link back to the **opportunity** it came from (so sold-versus-delivered stays connected end to end).
