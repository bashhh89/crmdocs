## What You Are Trying To Do

This track is for ad sales, graphics, content, feeds, sponsor relationships, and verification work.

You are learning how to:

1. **Track what ran** — placement records by sponsor, game, team, and <GlossaryTerm term="company" />. Every game a sponsor appears on the board is its own record, with a position, a date, and an execution status.
2. **Verify what was promised** — the monthly Nielsen and verification routine, tracked as records instead of a side spreadsheet, so nothing stays silently pending.
3. **Match contract to reality** — contracted games versus actual placements, per sponsor and per team, so under-delivery gets caught while there is still make-good inventory in the season.
4. **Read sponsor health** — the dashboard review across the whole relationship: placements, verification status, and contract pace in one view.

<Callout title="Where this lives">
Sponsor relationships, Companies, opportunities, placements, verifications, contracts, and dashboards all live in the CRM under the **Media & Sponsorships** section of the left navigation. Supporting creative or operations activity connects back through the Company record, so the account is always the anchor.
</Callout>

## Why the connections matter

The reason this beats spreadsheets is not the individual records — it is the links between them. A placement knows its sponsor, its home and away team, its position, and the opportunity it was sold on. A verification record knows its sponsor, league, and month. A contract knows its sponsor, team, and season. Because everything ties back to the same Company records, one question — "how is this sponsor doing?" — pulls the whole picture instead of three partial answers from three different files.

## Day 1 / Day 30 / Reference

- **Day 1:** find a sponsor, open placements, and identify what ran at last night's game.
- **Day 30:** reconcile contracted games, actual placements, verification status, and dashboard totals for a full monthly sponsor readout.
- **Reference as needed:** Nielsen verification routine, sponsor contract reads, dashboard review, creative and support context on the account.
