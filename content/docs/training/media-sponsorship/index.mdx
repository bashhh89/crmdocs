## What You Are Trying To Do

This track is for ad sales, graphics, content, feeds, sponsor relationships, and verification work.

You are learning how to:

1. **Track what ran** - placement records by sponsor, game, team, and Company.
2. **Verify what was promised** - monthly Nielsen and verification routines.
3. **Match contract to reality** - contracted games versus actual placements.
4. **Read sponsor health** - dashboard review across the relationship.

<Callout title="Where this lives">
Sponsor relationships, Companies, opportunities, placements, and dashboards live in the CRM. Supporting creative or operations activity may connect back through the Company record.
</Callout>

## Day 1 / Day 30 / Reference

- **Day 1:** find a sponsor, open placements, and identify what ran.
- **Day 30:** reconcile contracted games, actual placements, verification, and dashboard totals.
- **Reference as needed:** Nielsen verification, sponsor contracts, dashboard review, creative/support context.
