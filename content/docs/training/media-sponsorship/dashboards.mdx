**After this lesson** you'll be able to see your side's activity at a glance and pull up a sponsor's entire relationship in one shot.


## Your tab on the dashboard

There's a **Media & Sponsorship** view on the dashboards that rolls up your side's activity and performance — so you're looking at your world, not the whole company's.

**Or just ask:** *"Show me the Media & Sponsorship numbers."*

## The whole sponsor relationship in one place

This is the part that beats three separate spreadsheets. Open the **sponsor's account** and you see their placements, their verification, and their contracts together.

**Or just ask:** *"Give me everything on Tire Rack."*

## A few questions you'll ask all the time

- *"What's running tonight?"*
- *"Which sponsors are behind their contracted games?"*
- *"What verifications are still pending this month?"*
- *"Show me everything [sponsor] has running this season."*

Because it all connects — a placement knows its sponsor, account, verification, and the deal it came from — the answers are instant and current.

## You only learn your part

You don't need the technology or service workflows — just Media & Sponsorship. Everyone does the same Core basics, then only their own track.

## Now you try →

Open one sponsor's account and find all three on it: a placement, a verification, and a contract.

👉 **[Open the CRM](https://crm.ancsports.net)**

That's the Media & Sponsorship track. Anything you forget, just ask the assistant.
