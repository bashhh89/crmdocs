## Your tab on the dashboard

There's a **Media & Sponsorship** view on the dashboards that rolls up your side's activity and performance — deals, accounts, and the game schedule — so you're looking at your world, not the whole company's. Media & Sponsorship deals are tagged with your business unit, which is what keeps the pipeline views and totals scoped to your vertical.

The grouped deal views total up as you filter — change the filter, and the group counts and revenue sums recompute live. That means the number you read off a filtered view *is* the number, current as of now, not as of the last time someone refreshed an export.

## The whole sponsor relationship in one place

<WhereThisLives tool="crm" path="Companies → sponsor Company → Media & Sponsorship tab" />

This is the part that beats three separate spreadsheets. Open the **sponsor's account** and you see their placements, their verification records, and their contracts together:

1. Find the sponsor's **Company record**.
2. The **Home tab** leads with the active-sponsor picture — who's committed and for how many games.
3. The **Media & Sponsorship tab** carries the detail: placements and contracts, side by side.
4. Verification records for the sponsor sit on the same account, so the confirmed-versus-pending status is one click away.

The account views work from both sides of the business. Open a **team's** Company and you see every sponsor placed at that property — the full board picture for the venue. ## Running the monthly readout

Put the pieces together in this order and the sponsor conversation writes itself:

1. **What ran** — the sponsor's placements for the month, statuses honest ([Placements](/docs/training/media-sponsorship/placements)).
2. **What's confirmed** — the month's verification verdicts and any discrepancy notes ([Nielsen Verification](/docs/training/media-sponsorship/nielsen-verification)).
3. **Where the contract stands** — executed count against contracted games, per team ([Sponsor Contracts](/docs/training/media-sponsorship/sponsor-contracts)).
4. **What's next** — pending verifications, make-good needs, and follow-ups, flagged on the account.

That's the full readout: delivery, proof, pace, and plan — all from one account page.

## Common mistakes

<OhShit
  symptom="The dashboard number doesn't match what you expected, and the instinct is to distrust the dashboard."
  fix="Drill into the underlying records — the dashboard is a rollup, so a wrong-looking number almost always traces to a placement missing its sponsor link, a stale status, or a deal missing its business unit tag."
  prevention="Treat dashboards as mirrors, not sources. When the mirror looks wrong, fix the records it reflects — never pencil-correct the readout in a slide and move on."
/>

<OhShit
  symptom="A sponsor readout gets assembled by exporting three views into a spreadsheet and stitching them together by hand."
  fix="Open the sponsor's account instead — placements, verification, and contracts are already joined there, live."
  prevention="If you find yourself rebuilding a join in a spreadsheet, stop: the account page is the join. The manual version goes stale the moment you save it."

## What good looks like

- You answer "what's running tonight," "who's behind contract," and "what's pending verification" from the platform in under a minute each.
- Monthly sponsor readouts come straight off the account page — no side spreadsheets, no stitched exports.
- When a dashboard number surprises you, your reflex is to drill into records, not to distrust or overwrite the rollup.
- The team reads the same live numbers you do — one source, zero version drift.
