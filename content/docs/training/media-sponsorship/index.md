# Media & Sponsorship

This track is for everyone on the **Media & Sponsorship** side — ad sales, graphics, content, and feeds. Once you've finished [Core](/docs/training/core), this is built around how *your* side of the business works.

## Coming in your track

- **Placements** — tracking sponsor placements game by game.
- **Nielsen verification** — your monthly verification workflow.
- **Sponsor contracts** — contracted games and seasons per sponsor and team.
- **Your dashboards** — the Media & Sponsorship view of revenue and activity.

📣 **Lessons are being added here.** In the meantime, [Core](/docs/training/core) gets you fully set up, and the assistant can already help with questions about your placements and sponsors.
