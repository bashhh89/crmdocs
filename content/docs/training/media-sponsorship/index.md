# Media & Sponsorship

This track is for everyone on the **Media & Sponsorship** side — ad sales, graphics, content, and feeds. You've finished [Core](/docs/training/core); now this shows you the CRM through *your* lens.

## What you'll learn here

1. **[Placements](/docs/training/media-sponsorship/placements)** — finding and logging sponsor placements by game, sponsor, and team.
2. **[Nielsen Verification](/docs/training/media-sponsorship/nielsen-verification)** — your monthly verification routine and keeping the trail clean.
3. **[Sponsor Contracts](/docs/training/media-sponsorship/sponsor-contracts)** — contracted games versus what's actually running.
4. **[Your Dashboards](/docs/training/media-sponsorship/dashboards)** — the whole sponsor relationship in one place.

The big idea: **everything connects.** A placement knows its sponsor, its account, its verification, and the deal it came from — so the picture stays whole and current, instead of living across spreadsheets that drift apart.
