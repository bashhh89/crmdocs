Welcome 👋 This is a short, self-paced course to get you comfortable in the CRM — at your own speed, on any device. No prior experience needed, and there are no wrong answers.

## How it works

**Two simple steps:**

1. **Do _Core_ first** — it's for everyone. Logging in, finding your way around, the day-to-day basics, and how to just *ask* the assistant to do things for you.
2. **Then do your vertical** — the part built around how *your* side of the business actually works.

<Callout title="Not sure where to start?">
Take the 2-minute intro chat first — it asks a few friendly questions and points you straight to the lessons that fit you. (Your personal link comes from Natalia.)
</Callout>

## What each lesson looks like

Every lesson is short and shows you the same thing two ways:

- **Click it** — the step-by-step path on screen.
- **Or just ask** — the same thing, done by typing a plain-English request to the assistant.

Pick whichever feels easier. Both get you to the same place.
