## Start With The Situation

Pick the path by the problem in front of you:

- **A client asks for pricing.** Open the deal, check the Company, confirm <abbr title="Where the deal sits in the bid flow: RFP Received, Scoping, Bid Submitted, Shortlisted, Won, Lost, or No Bid.">Bid Status</abbr>, and move into proposal work.
- **A venue has an issue before an event.** Open the Company, check tickets and recent activity, then use the service track.
- **An RFP lands with a deadline.** Triage the RFP, price the deal, generate the SOW, and make sure the files attach back to the CRM opportunity.
- **Leadership asks for a number.** Ask the assistant, then open the records behind the answer before sharing it.

## The standard

Use the CRM with the same standard you would use for a client-facing proposal or an internal revenue review:

- **Find the right record before creating a new one.** Duplicate Companies create bad history, bad reporting, and wasted follow-up.
- **Keep status fields current.** Stage, owner, close date, amount, bid status, service status, and next step drive dashboards.
- **Write notes that someone else can use.** A good note says what happened, what changed, and what happens next.
- **Use views instead of personal spreadsheets.** If the information belongs to the team, it belongs in a shared CRM view or dashboard.
- **Ask the assistant for speed, then verify important numbers.** The assistant is excellent for finding, summarizing, and drafting. For revenue, dates, and client commitments, check the source record.

## Common Questions

<details>
  <summary>Which app do I use?</summary>
  <p>Use the ANC app, not Standard. If dashboards or views look missing, check the top-left app switcher.</p>
</details>

<details>
  <summary>Where do I log an RFP?</summary>
  <p>Log it on the CRM Opportunity with Bid Status set to RFP Received.</p>
</details>

<details>
  <summary>How do I mark pricing done?</summary>
  <p>Use Pricing Complete on the CRM opportunity, or generate the one-pager in the Proposal Engine when that workflow applies.</p>
</details>

<details>
  <summary>Where do tickets live?</summary>
  <p>Service Dashboard creates and manages tickets. The CRM shows linked ticket history for Company context.</p>
</details>

## What each lesson gives you

Each lesson is built around real work:

- **What you are trying to accomplish** - the business reason behind the workflow.
- **Where to look** - the exact CRM area, view, dashboard, or Company page.
- **How to do it by clicking** - the repeatable path for normal use.
- **How to ask the assistant** - the same action in plain English.
- **What good looks like** - the record quality standard before you move on.
