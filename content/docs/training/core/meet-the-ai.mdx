**After this lesson** you'll be able to use the assistant for common CRM work: finding records, summarizing accounts, checking missing fields, drafting notes, pulling numbers, and getting to the records behind a report.

## The big idea

The CRM has a built-in assistant called **Scout**. Here's the only thing you need to believe:

> **If you can send a text message, you can use it.**

You don't learn commands. You don't memorize anything. You type what you want in plain English, the way you'd ask a colleague — and it does it.

## Where it is

Look for the **Ask AI** button (top of the screen). Click it, type your request, hit enter.

## What the assistant is best at

Use the assistant when you want speed across the CRM:

- *"Open the Knicks account."*
- *"Create an opportunity for the Phoenix Suns."*
- *"What's due this week?"*
- *"Move this deal to won."*
- *"How much have we won this quarter?"*
- *"Show me my open opportunities."*
- *"Make me a report of closed-won deals this month."*
- *"Find accounts with missing owners."*
- *"Summarize the last activity on this account."*
- *"Show me the deals behind this forecast number."*

It opens the record, makes the change, or pulls the number — right there in the sidebar.

## The one rule to remember

**Anything you can click, you can also just ask.** Throughout the rest of the course, every lesson shows you both ways. Over time you'll find you reach for the assistant first — it's faster, and it's the same wherever you are, even on your phone.

<Callout title="It's okay to experiment">
You can't break anything by asking a question. If a request isn't clear, the assistant will just ask you what you meant. Try things.
</Callout>

## Ask better questions

Good prompts include the record, the action, and the output you want.

Weak:

```text
Show me pipeline.
```

Better:

```text
Show me my open Technology opportunities closing this quarter, grouped by stage, and list any missing close dates.
```

Weak:

```text
Clean this account.
```

Better:

```text
Review this account for duplicate companies, missing contacts, stale opportunities, missing owner fields, and notes without next steps.
```

## Use it for quality control

The assistant is not only for shortcuts. It is also a second set of eyes.

Try these checks:

- *"What important fields are missing on this opportunity?"*
- *"Does this account have duplicate contacts?"*
- *"Which of my deals have no next step?"*
- *"Which opportunities changed stage this week?"*
- *"Find closed-won deals with missing contract value."*
- *"Summarize this account for a leadership review."*

## Use it for numbers, then verify

For reporting, use this pattern:

1. Ask for the number.
2. Ask which records were included.
3. Open the source records.
4. Check dates, amount, status, and business unit.
5. Only then send or present the number.

**Example:** *"How much Technology revenue is booked this year, and show me the opportunities included in that total."*

<Callout title="Important">
The assistant can get you to an answer quickly. For revenue, contract values, dates, client promises, and forecast calls, always verify the records behind the answer before sharing it.
</Callout>
