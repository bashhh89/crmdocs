## 1. Logging in

1. Go to **[crm.ancsports.net](https://crm.ancsports.net)**.
2. Sign in with your work email.
3. If you land on a plain, empty-looking screen, look at the **top-left** and switch to the **ANC** workspace — that's where all your views and folders live.

## 2. The layout

Once you're in, here's what you're looking at:

- **Left side — the menu.** This is how you move around: Opportunities, Companies, People, and your saved views. Click any of them to open that list.
- **The middle — the list.** Rows of records (for example, all your opportunities), with columns across the top.
- **Open a record.** Click any row to open its full page — all its details, history, and related items in one place.
- **The assistant button.** Look for the **Ask AI** button — that's the assistant (we call it **Scout**). You type a plain-English request and it does the work. More on that in [Meet the Assistant](/docs/training/core/meet-the-ai).

![The CRM layout — the menu down the left, your list of records in the middle, and columns across the top](/img/screenshots/twenty-crm/01-companies.png)

That's the whole shape of it: **menu on the left, a list in the middle, columns across the top.** Every area — Companies, People, Opportunities — looks like this, so once you know one, you know them all.

## One thing that trips people up

What the old system called **Opportunities** is shown here the same way — **Opportunities**. Same thing you've always worked with, same word. Nothing new to learn there.

## 3. Make it yours — favorites

You don't have to hunt for the same things every day:

1. Open a record or a view you use a lot.
2. Click the **star** to add it to your **Favorites**.
3. It now sits at the top of your menu — one click away, every time.

**Or just ask:** type *"add this to my favorites"* to the assistant.

## Now you try →

Open the CRM, switch to the ANC workspace, click into **Opportunities**, open any record, and star one view you think you'll use.
