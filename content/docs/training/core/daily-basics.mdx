Every task below is shown **two ways**. Use whichever feels easier — both get you to the same result.

![The Opportunities list — each row is a deal, with its company, business unit, and status in columns you can sort and filter](/img/screenshots/twenty-crm/02-opportunities.png)

That list above is where most days start. Everything below is just acting on it.

## 1. Create an opportunity

- **Click it:** open **Opportunities** → press **New record** (top right) → fill in the name and a couple of details → it saves automatically.
- **Or just ask:** *"Create a new opportunity for the Boston Red Sox."*

## 2. Find a record

- **Click it:** use the **search bar** at the top — type a company name or the opportunity number.
- **Or just ask:** *"Open the Dodgers account for me."*

## 3. Open an account

- **Click it:** go to **Companies** → click the company → its page shows every opportunity, contact, ticket, and note in one place.
- **Or just ask:** *"Show me everything on Madison Square Garden."*

## 4. Update a stage or status

- **Click it:** open the opportunity → change the **Stage** field to the new step.
- **Or just ask:** *"Move this deal to proposal stage."*

## 5. Edit a field

- **Click it:** open the record → click straight into the field you want (close date, amount, etc.) → type → it saves.
- **Or just ask:** *"Set the close date on this to March 15."*

## 6. Save to favorites

- **Click it:** open a record or view → click the **star** → it pins to the top of your menu.
- **Or just ask:** *"Add this to my favorites."*

## 7. Switch between views

- **Click it:** at the top of any list, open the **view dropdown** (e.g. "All Opportunities") and pick a saved view — Pipeline, Backlog, and others.
- **Or just ask:** *"Show me the proposal pipeline."*

## 8. See only your accounts

- **Click it:** open a list → add a **filter** for owner = you → save it as your own view. Now you only see your accounts, not everyone's.
- **Or just ask:** *"Show me only my open opportunities."*

## Now you try →

Pick any three from this list and do them in the real CRM — once each way (click, then ask). That's the whole daily toolkit.
