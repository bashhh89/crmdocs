# Executive Summary

<ToolBadge tool="crm" />

Use this path when you need the number, the records behind it, and the confidence to share it.

<Shot src="/img/screenshots/crm/t-dashboard-overview.png" caption="Leadership reads the rollup, then drills into the records that create the number." />

## Start Here

1. Open the dashboard.
2. Filter by business unit, owner, date, or league.
3. Ask Scout for the record list behind the number.
4. Open the records and verify stage, amount, close date, owner, and Bid Status.

```text
Show this quarter's open pipeline by vertical, grouped by stage, and flag missing close dates.
```

```text
Show the records behind this forecast total so I can verify the number.
```
