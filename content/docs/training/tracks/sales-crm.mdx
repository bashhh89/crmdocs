# Sales & Account Management

<ToolBadge tool="crm" />

Move deals from RFP to won, manage Company records, and keep forecast numbers accurate.

## Scenario 1: The RFP Just Landed

<ToolBadge tool="crm" />

> *It is 9 AM. Your AE forwards an RFP from Louisville Athletics for a stadium LED install. Due date is April 30. You need to log this before the 10 AM standup.*

### Step 1: Check If The Company Exists

Open CRM -> Companies -> search **Louisville Athletics**.

<WhereThisLives tool="crm" path="Companies -> Search" />

**Why this matters:** creating duplicates creates merge work and splits history across multiple records.

**Scout shortcut:**

```text
Log a new RFP: Louisville Athletics, TECHNOLOGY, due April 30, AE [your name].
```

### Step 2: Create The Opportunity

If the Company exists, open it -> Opportunities tab -> New.

Set:

- stage = PROPOSAL
- bidStatus = RFP_RECEIVED
- businessUnit = TECHNOLOGY
- proposalDueDate = April 30
- league = NCAA

**Common mistake:** leaving stage as New instead of Proposal. The deal may not show where the team expects it in the bid flow.

### Step 3: Verify It Appears In Bid Tracker

Open Bid Tracker and confirm the opportunity appears in the RFP Received column.

<WhereThisLives tool="crm" path="Views -> Bid Tracker" />

## Scenario 2: Moving To Pricing

<ToolBadge tool="connected" />

> *The scope is finalized. You have told the estimator the requirements. Now you need to mark this ready for pricing.*

Open the Opportunity and confirm <GlossaryTerm term="pricingComplete" /> is set when pricing output is generated or ready for proposal movement.

**What happens:** the pricing status and proposal due date help Natalia's estimation/proposal queue stay clean.

**Scout check:**

```text
Show deals where Pricing Complete is false and proposal due date is this week.
```

<WhereThisLives tool="crm" path="Opportunity -> Pricing Complete / Proposal Due Date" />

## Scenario 3: The Deal Won

<ToolBadge tool="crm" />

> *Client signed. Now you need the revenue and forecast fields to reflect the win.*

Record the value, close date, owner, and stage. If the deal splits across fiscal years, confirm how the revenue split should be captured before reporting it.

**Finance note:** multi-year or mid-year split deals need extra care before leadership uses the number.
