# Venue Services

This track is for everyone on the **Venue Services** side — service contracts, maintenance, and field operations. Once you've finished [Core](/docs/training/core), this is built around how *your* day actually runs.

## Coming in your track

- **Service tickets** — finding, tracking, and updating tickets, and how they connect to the account.
- **Your account view** — seeing every venue, contract, and open item in one place.
- **Day-to-day workflow** — the views and habits that keep your work flowing.
