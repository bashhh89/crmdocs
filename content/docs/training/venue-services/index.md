# Venue Services

This track is for everyone on the **Venue Services** side — service contracts, maintenance, and field operations. You've finished [Core](/docs/training/core); now this shows you the CRM through *your* lens — built around how your day actually runs.

## What you'll learn here

1. **[Service Tickets](/docs/training/venue-services/service-tickets)** — creating, assigning, tracking, and escalating tickets, and how they tie to the account.
2. **[The Account View](/docs/training/venue-services/the-account-view)** — seeing every venue, contact, contract, and open item in one place.
3. **[Venue Activity](/docs/training/venue-services/venue-activity)** — history, logging site visits, and recurring service.
4. **[Your Day-to-Day](/docs/training/venue-services/your-day-to-day)** — the views and habits that keep nothing falling through the cracks.

The big idea: **everything for a venue lives on its account.** No more hunting across spreadsheets and inboxes to figure out the state of a site.
