**After this lesson** you'll be able to open any venue and see its whole picture — service, people, scope — before a call or a visit.

## Everything for a venue, in one place

Open the venue's **company** record and you see it all: its service activity, its open tickets, its contacts, and its history — together, not scattered.

**Or just ask:** *"Show me everything on Madison Square Garden."*

## The people you work with

The venue's **people** are listed right on its account — contacts, roles, the works. Handy before you reach out about a follow-up.

**Or just ask:** *"Who are the contacts at TD Garden?"*

## The scope behind the service

The service work ties to the venue's account, where the related **deal and scope** live — so you can connect what you're doing on the ground to what was actually sold.

**Or just ask:** *"Pull up the account for TD Garden before my visit."*

## One company, multiple venues

A company can have **more than one venue or site** associated with it, and the service activity ties to the right one. So a multi-property client still rolls up cleanly under the account.

<Callout title="Picking up new work">
When a deal is won for a venue you cover, it ties to that account — so the new work shows up right where you're already looking. The account carries it from the sales side straight to you.
</Callout>

## Now you try →

Open one of your venues and find three things on it: an open item, a contact, and the deal/scope behind the service.
