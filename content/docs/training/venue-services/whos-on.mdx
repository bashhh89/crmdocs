## Open Who's On

1. In the sidebar, open **Events & Schedule**.
2. Choose **Who's On**.

The page opens on the schedule that is out, with the day card set to today when today is covered, or to the schedule's first day when it is not.

<WhereThisLives tool="services" path="Services dashboard → Events & Schedule → Who's On" />

<Callout type="info">
The top line names the schedule that is out and the day it was published. If today is not covered yet, it says so and tells you when the schedule starts.
</Callout>

## Read the day card

The day card shows one day at a time — one row per shift line, with the name on it:

- the day lines (TS 8am-4pm, TS 10am-6pm, TS 2pm-10pm) and the night line for that day of the week;
- **Field Ops Travel**, with the location beside the name;
- **Vacation**, so you can see who is out.

Use the **‹ ›** arrows to move a day at a time. If a line still needs someone, it shows how many spots are open. Once today is on the schedule, **Back to today** returns you to it.

## Read the two-week grid

Below the day card is the full two-week grid: one row per shift line, one column per day. It is the same picture the team used to receive as a screenshot, except it is live.

- Click any **day header** in the grid and the day card jumps to that day.
- Your own name is highlighted wherever you appear, so you can find yourself at a glance.

<WhereThisLives tool="services" path="Who's On → Week 1 / Week 2 → click a day header" />

## Put your shifts in Outlook

In the top corner of Who's On, beside **My availability**, is **Add to Outlook**. Click it and a panel opens with a calendar link that is yours alone. You subscribe once; every schedule that goes out after that shows up in your calendar on its own, with nothing to re-add.

1. In Outlook, open the calendar and choose **Add calendar → Subscribe from web** (in the desktop app: **Open Calendar → From Internet**).
2. Back on Who's On, click **Copy link**, paste it into Outlook, and call the calendar **ANC Tech Support**.

Under the link box:

- **Open in my calendar app** hands the link straight to your calendar app.
- **Download this schedule (.ics)** gives you a one-off file. It will not update — use the subscription for that.
- **Reset link** makes a new link and the old one stops working. Use it if your link ever gets shared.

The link shows only your own shifts on published schedules: a day shift as a timed event, a night line ending after midnight, and Travel or Vacation days as all-day entries with the location.

## Master schedule in Outlook

Schedule managers can subscribe to **everyone’s published shifts**, with the technician’s name on each entry. Your personal subscription keeps its own link.

1. On **Who’s on**, open **Add to Outlook**.
2. In **Calendar**, choose **Master schedule — everyone’s shifts**.
3. Select **Copy link**.
4. In Outlook on the web, choose **Add calendar → Subscribe from web**. Paste the link, name it **ANC Tech Support — Master Schedule**, and subscribe.

From the **Support Tech Schedule** board, **Master schedule to Outlook** opens this panel directly.

<WhereThisLives tool="services" path="Support Tech Schedule → Master schedule to Outlook" />

<Callout type="info">
**Published shifts only.** Draft schedules and availability responses are excluded. Outlook controls refresh timing, and updates can take more than 24 hours; check Who’s on for the latest published changes. Downloading a file gives you a one-off copy rather than a subscription.
</Callout>

Keep the link private. It works while your account is active and you have schedule access. **Reset link** replaces only the selected calendar’s link, and Outlook will need that new link. Your other subscription is unaffected. Switching the Calendar selector does not change a subscription already added to Outlook.

## Go to your availability and back

- **My availability** in the corner takes you to your own yes-or-no page.
- On that page, pick the schedule that is out and a **See who's on** link brings you straight back to the board.

### Your answered days change colour

On My Availability, a day changes colour the moment you answer it, so what is left to fill is visible at a glance:

- **green** when you said Available;
- **grey** when you said Off;
- a **lighter green** when only some of the lines on that day have an answer;
- **white** when nothing has been said yet.

The count at the top of the page keeps score — "3 of 7 days answered" — so you can see when you are done.

<WhereThisLives tool="services" path="Services dashboard → Events & Schedule → My Availability" />

<Callout type="warn">
Nothing on Who's On can be edited. It is the published schedule, read-only, and it updates the moment the board changes. Availability is still answered on My Availability, and the schedule itself is built and published by the support-schedule manager.
</Callout>

## Planning, requests and notifications

The scheduling tools cover the full planning cycle: collect availability, build a draft,
review coverage, publish, and manage later changes. Open the tools from the links above
the board, Who’s on, or My availability.

### See the whole month

Open **Month calendar**, choose a month, and filter by technician, shift, or assignment
location. Published assignments are blue. Schedule builders also see amber draft
assignments; technicians see published schedules only. On a phone, the month becomes
a readable day-by-day list.

For separate Outlook calendars, open **Who’s on → Add to Outlook** and choose a named
technician. Copy their link and subscribe from web in Outlook. Add each technician
separately, then use Outlook’s calendar list to show or hide them. Each subscription
has its own link, and resetting one leaves the others unchanged.

### Start a draft from the previous schedule

Choose **+ New schedule**, set the first date and length, and select a previous schedule
under **Start from**. Leave it on **Blank schedule** to start fresh.

The new draft checks the technician’s latest availability, active crew and shift settings,
and weekly limits. Unanswered availability carries into the draft with a publishing warning,
so you can collect responses after opening the schedule. Known unavailability and rule
conflicts stay open. Locations are left blank for rechecking. **Change history & acknowledgments** retains the
copy results and reasons, so you can see what needs attention before publishing.

### Set an availability deadline

Open **Availability deadline & reminders**, choose the deadline, and select **Save deadline**.
The deadline is the end of that day in Eastern time.

With **Automatic reminders** enabled, reminders run at **9am Eastern**, beginning the day
before the deadline. Only technicians with unanswered days receive them. Partial responses
remain incomplete; reminders stop when the technician finishes or the schedule is published.
**Remind missing respondents** sends a reminder on demand. Both options share a limit of one
reminder per person, per schedule, per day.

### Review before publishing

**Review & publish** opens **Workload & publishing review**. It shows each technician’s hours,
days, untimed entries, and days per week. Untimed travel or vacation entries do not count as
24 worked hours. These are planning totals, not payroll calculations.

Resolve assignment conflicts before publishing. Review any open coverage and availability
warnings, confirm that you have considered them, then choose **Publish reviewed schedule**.
If the schedule changes after your review, review the latest version before publishing.

### Handle time off and swaps

Technicians open **Requests & approvals → Submit a request**, choose **Time off** or **Shift swap**,
fill the dates or both published shifts, and include a reason.

A swap first goes to the other technician. After they choose **Accept swap**, a schedule builder
can approve it. The shifts and both technicians’ availability and weekly limits are checked
again before the exchange. A changed shift requires a fresh request.

For time off, the approval step shows how many assigned shifts would be removed. Confirm that
impact before approval; the removed coverage becomes open. Approved time off cannot be
replaced by an availability response. A builder can cancel approved time off with a reason;
previously removed assignments stay open until they are deliberately reassigned.

### Receive notifications from technicians

Open **Scheduling inbox** from the schedule tools or the sidebar. Availability changes and
approval requests go to the schedule builders. Changes to published shifts go to the affected
technicians, who can choose **Acknowledge change**.

Turn on **Email alerts** to receive new updates at your account email. Emails are grouped and
checked every five minutes. The scheduling inbox retains updates whether email is on or off;
email is initially off for each person.

On the board, **Change history & acknowledgments** records the previous and current shifts,
who changed them, and each technician’s acknowledgment. Reading an update and acknowledging
a shift change are separate actions.
