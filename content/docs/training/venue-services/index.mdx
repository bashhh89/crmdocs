## What You Are Trying To Do

This track is for service contracts, maintenance, field operations, and venue support.

You are learning how to:

1. **Track what's broken** - create, assign, escalate, and close tickets.
2. **See the full venue story** - Company, contacts, contracts, opportunities, tickets, notes, and history.
3. **Check venue health** - recent activity, recurring service, event context, and outstanding issues.
4. **Keep the handoff clean** - make sure the next person can see status without hunting.

<Callout title="Where this lives">
The Company relationship and service history live in the CRM. Day-to-day tickets, events, technician work, and venue operations live in the Service Dashboard. Synced history comes back to the CRM Company view.
</Callout>

## Day 1 / Day 30 / Reference

- **Day 1:** open the Company, find open tickets, check event context, and read the latest note.
- **Day 30:** spot venue health patterns, overdue issues, repeated failures, and handoff gaps.
- **Reference as needed:** IoT Operations Workspace, walkthroughs, maintenance records, reports, portals.

## Quick Glossary

- <abbr title="An issue raised against a venue and tracked until assigned, resolved, and closed.">Service Ticket</abbr> - an issue tied to a venue or Company.
- <abbr title="A game, concert, or service event at a venue.">Event</abbr> - scheduled venue activity.
- <abbr title="A venue site visit by an ANC technician, usually with issues, notes, or readiness checks.">Walkthrough</abbr> - a site visit or readiness check.
- <abbr title="The spreadsheet-style Field Ops workspace for physical venue data, displays, rack/device notes, walkthrough logs, maintenance rows, forms, and operational documents.">IoT Operations Workspace</abbr> - the structured operations workspace for venue data.
