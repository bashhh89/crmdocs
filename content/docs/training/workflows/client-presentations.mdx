## The pages you start from

Every presentation is assembled from finished ANC page templates — you pick the pages, you don't design them.

- **Cover** — the client's name and venue over an ANC photograph.
- **Contents** — the chapter list. Click any chapter and the deck jumps straight to it. The same chapters sit across the top of every page.
- **Your Team** — the people accountable for the project. Each portrait opens their bio in place.
- **Text and images** — a capability list beside a photo set that pages through without leaving the page.
- **Pop-up page** — tiles that open for the detail.

## Pop-ups

A pop-up can be **plain text**, **text with an image**, **an image on its own**, **a video**, or **a PDF**. They exist so a page can stay clean and still carry the depth a client asks for in the room.

Press Escape or the close button to come back to the page you were on.

## Sharing and printing

Publishing a deck produces a **tracked client link**. The link is the deliverable — it keeps working, and it records who opened it.

The **Print** button at the top of every page produces the whole deck as a PDF, one slide per sheet. It is the same PDF whether you print it from inside the app or a client prints it from the link you sent.

<Callout type="info">
  The client link and the PDF always reflect the version you published — not whatever you happen to be editing. Publish again when you want the client to see your latest changes.
</Callout>

## Seeing who read it

Every deck has an analytics page: how many people opened it, where from, how far they got, how long they stayed, and a recording of each viewing you can play back.

That is the whole loop — build, share, and see who read it.
