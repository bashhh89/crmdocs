```text
Email / External
  Client sends RFP
        |
        v
CRM
  Log Opportunity: stage=PROPOSAL, Bid Status=RFP_RECEIVED
        |
        v
CRM / Scout
  Analyze RFP and extract requirements
        |
        v
Proposal Engine
  Price in Excel
        |
        v
Proposal Engine
  Mirror Mode -> One-Pager Generated
        |
        v
CRM
  Pricing Complete = TRUE, proposal attached to Files
        |
        v
Proposal Engine
  Generate SOW: Premium + Installation
        |
        v
CRM
  SOW attached to Deal
        |
        v
CRM
  Move to BID_SUBMITTED in Bid Tracker
        |
        v
External
  Client review
        |
        v
CRM
  Mark WON or LOST
```

## Decision Point

Is this a Venue Services deal?

- **Yes:** include Service Dashboard fulfillment, event staffing, tickets, and venue operations.
- **No:** continue through CRM and Proposal Engine only.

```text
Analyze this RFP and extract requirements, due date, league, business unit, and missing information.
```
