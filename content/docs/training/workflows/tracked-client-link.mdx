## A client never gets the file

Open the deck in the builder and select **Share** at the top. The client receives a link, not an attachment: the link is yours to name, watch and revoke, and the deck behind it is the frozen published copy, so later edits never change what they already opened.

## Share checks the deck first

Before anything goes out, Share reads every page. If template text or another client's name is still on a slide, it names the slide and stops. Fix the page, then come back. The same check runs inside Preflight as **Client-ready content**.

## Name the link, decide on identity

- **Link name** — say who it is for (`Riverside Arena — GM review`). One deck can carry several links, each with its own analytics.
- **Ask viewers for name and email** — switch it on when you need to know exactly who opened the deck. The client enters their name, work email and company once; every page they reach and every decision they take is tied to that name.

Select **Go live, create link and copy**. The deck is published, the link is created, and it is already on your clipboard. Every link appears under **Existing links** with **Copy**, open and **Revoke**.

<Callout type="info">
  Revoke is immediate. A revoked link shows the client a branded "this link isn't valid" page and nothing else; the deck itself stays live for every other link.
</Callout>

## Prove it with Preflight

Open the **⋯** menu and select **Preflight check**, then **Run preflight**. **Ready to send** means all seven checks passed against the published version:

- the saved builder matches the published version;
- the frozen client copy is intact;
- a valid client link points to the deck;
- the content is client-ready — a client name is set and no template placeholders remain;
- every referenced image, video and PDF loads;
- every slide opens cleanly on desktop and on a phone;
- the published PDF has one page per slide.

If a check is blocked, fix the reported item, Share again to republish, and rerun Preflight.

## What the client sees

With identity on, the link opens on the Decision Room gate: name, work email, company, then **Open Decision Room**. The deck runs in the browser with chapter navigation, and the **Respond** bar lets the client approve, ask a question or request a revision without leaving the presentation.

## Read the analytics

From the dashboard row select the analytics icon, or use **Analytics** in the builder's **⋯** menu. The page shows viewers, average time, who reached the end, the biggest drop-off, devices, and decisions, with a bar per page showing how far each viewer actually got. **Export analytics CSV** on the dashboard row gives you the same data as a spreadsheet with the viewer's name and company on every row.

Your own opens are never counted, so checking the link yourself does not inflate the client's numbers.
