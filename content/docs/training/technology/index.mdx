## What You Are Trying To Do

This track is for everyone who owns or supports Technology deals: LED displays, LiveSync, processing, products, pricing, and proposal movement.

You are learning how to:

1. **Find the Company before creating anything new** - avoid duplicate Companies and split history.
2. **Keep pipeline clean** - make stage, owner, close date, amount, and next step obvious.
3. **Price a deal** - move from CRM opportunity to estimate/proposal work.
4. **Prepare a client-facing package** - understand where one-pagers, SOWs, and proposal artifacts attach.
5. **Read your dashboard** - know what is booked, forecasted, stale, or missing.

## How a Technology deal actually flows

Every deal on this desk moves through the same bid flow, and the whole company reads its position from one field — <GlossaryTerm term="bidStatus" />:

**RFP Received → Scoping → Bid Submitted → Shortlisted → Won / Lost / No Bid**

Two systems carry the deal through that flow, and the handoff between them is the part most new team members get wrong:

- **The CRM** holds the record of truth: the Company, the opportunity, the owner, the amounts, the due dates, and every document that has ever gone to the client.
- **The Proposal Engine** is where pricing happens: estimates, one-pagers, SOWs, and full proposals. When you generate a file there, it attaches itself to the matching CRM opportunity — that is <GlossaryTerm term="universalCrmPush" /> doing its job. Nobody uploads anything by hand.

The practical consequence: if your CRM record is clean, everything downstream — the proposal queue, the forecast, the leadership dashboard — is clean too. If your record is stale, you are the reason a number on someone else's screen is wrong. That is the whole discipline of this track in one sentence.

- **Reference as needed:** proposal artifacts, SOW handoff, dashboard interpretation, win/loss cleanup.

## The daily ten minutes

The habit that separates a clean operator from someone who gets chased for status updates is a short, fixed pass every morning:

1. Open **Proposal Pipeline** — it is sorted by what is due, so the top of the list is your day.
2. Scan your deals for anything missing: owner, close date, amount, next step, Bid Status.
3. Fix what you see. A blank due date or a stale stage takes ten seconds to correct and hours to chase later.
4. Glance at the **Technology** tab of the company dashboard to confirm your changes rolled up.

Ten minutes. That is the whole tax. Everything else in this track builds on that pass.

## Quick Glossary

- <GlossaryTerm term="bidStatus" /> - where the deal sits in the bid flow: RFP Received, Scoping, Bid Submitted, Shortlisted, Won, Lost, or No Bid.
- <GlossaryTerm term="pricingComplete" /> - the deal has generated pricing output, ready for review or client movement.
- <GlossaryTerm term="mirrorMode" /> - the Proposal Engine mode that reproduces pricing documents exactly, line for line.
- <GlossaryTerm term="universalCrmPush" /> - generated files land on the right CRM deal automatically, no manual upload.
- <abbr title="Scope of Work: the document defining what ANC will deliver.">SOW</abbr> - what ANC will deliver.
- <abbr title="Request For Proposal: the client's document asking ANC to bid on work.">RFP</abbr> - the client's bid request.
