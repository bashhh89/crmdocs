## What You Are Trying To Do

This track is for everyone who owns or supports Technology deals: LED displays, LiveSync, processing, products, pricing, and proposal movement.

You are learning how to:

1. **Find the Company before creating anything new** - avoid duplicate Companies and split history.
2. **Keep pipeline clean** - make stage, owner, close date, amount, and next step obvious.
3. **Price a deal** - move from CRM opportunity to estimate/proposal work.
4. **Prepare a client-facing package** - understand where one-pagers, SOWs, and proposal artifacts attach.
5. **Read your dashboard** - know what is booked, forecasted, stale, or missing.

<Callout title="Where this lives">
The opportunity, Company, owner, stage, <abbr title="Where a deal sits in the bid flow: RFP Received, Scoping, Bid Submitted, Shortlisted, Won, Lost, or No Bid.">Bid Status</abbr>, and dashboard live in the CRM. Pricing and proposal generation live in the Proposal Engine. Generated files attach back to the CRM opportunity.
</Callout>

## Day 1 / Day 30 / Reference

- **Day 1:** find a Company, open an opportunity, understand stage, owner, amount, close date, and next step.
- **Day 30:** keep your pipeline clean, know which deals need pricing, and explain what changed this week.
- **Reference as needed:** proposal artifacts, SOW handoff, dashboard interpretation, win/loss cleanup.

## Quick Glossary

- <abbr title="Where a deal sits in the bid flow: RFP Received, Scoping, Bid Submitted, Shortlisted, Won, Lost, or No Bid.">Bid Status</abbr> - where the deal sits in the bid flow.
- <abbr title="A flag that means proposal pricing has been generated and is ready for review or client movement.">Pricing Complete</abbr> - the deal has generated pricing output.
- <abbr title="Scope of Work: the document defining what ANC will deliver.">SOW</abbr> - what ANC will deliver.
- <abbr title="Request For Proposal: the client's document asking ANC to bid on work.">RFP</abbr> - the client's bid request.
