## Where deadlines live

Every opportunity has a **proposal due date**, and there are views that sort by what's coming up — so deadlines are in front of you instead of relying on memory.

1. Open the **view dropdown** at the top of the Opportunities list.
2. Pick **Proposal Pipeline** — it's sorted by what's due.
3. Make that your landing spot each morning.

**Or just ask:** *"What proposals are due this week?"*

<Callout title="Start your day here">
If you make the due-date view your home screen, the system carries the reminders so you don't have to. One glance tells you what needs attention today.
</Callout>

## Marking pricing complete

When a deal's pricing is finished, set the **pricing complete** field on the opportunity. That's the signal the proposal team watches — it moves the deal into the "ready" views so everyone knows it's cleared to go.

**Or just ask:** *"Mark pricing complete on the Rangers deal."*

## The home base for proposal work

The **Estimation & Proposals** view mirrors the whole workflow — what's being scoped, what's priced, what's ready — at a glance. It's the daily base for the proposal side. (More on the flow itself in [Estimation & Proposals](/docs/training/technology/estimation-and-proposals).)

## Now you try →

Open **Proposal Pipeline**, find the next proposal due, and on one deal that's ready, set **pricing complete**.
