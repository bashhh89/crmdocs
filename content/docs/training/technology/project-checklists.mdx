## Build a checklist from a proposal

Under **Build & estimate**, open **Project Checklists**. Choose **From a proposal** and search by client, project or venue. Each result shows what the proposal holds — its displays, responsibility lines, priced sections, scope of work and notes. Pick the proposal and select **Build checklist**.

The checklist reads the proposal's displays, the priced work under each display, the responsibility matrix, the scope of work and the notes. It takes about a minute; the page updates on its own.

A proposal with none of those recorded says so in the list. Upload its scope document instead.

## Read where each item came from

Items are grouped by phase: contract and kickoff, engineering and permits, procurement, site preparation, structure and electrical, installation, control system, testing, and closeout. The phase list on the left jumps to each phase and shows how many items in it are done.

- **From the scope** under an item shows the exact sentence it was built from.
- **Owner** comes straight from the responsibility matrix wherever the matrix marks who does the work: ANC or the client. Lines the proposal states as ANC's assumptions become items to confirm on site.
- **By others** marks anything the scope excludes, so you can confirm who is covering it.
- Filter by **Owner** or by **Display / area** to see one side of the job at a time.

## Track the job

Tick items as they are done. The checklist records who ticked each item and when, and progress updates for everyone. Items owned by others are listed but not counted in progress.

Change an owner, edit the wording of an item, or add a step in any phase. Changes save as you go; if someone else saved a moment before you, the checklist shows their version and asks you to make your change again.

## Take it with you

- **Excel** downloads a working sheet with **Done** and **Owner** dropdowns and a live count of items done.
- **PDF** is the checklist phase by phase, with a box beside every item.

## Build from an uploaded scope

Choose **Upload a scope** to build from a Word, PDF or text file, or **Paste the scope** to paste the text. Say who the scope is written for — an installation subcontractor or ANC's client — and select **Build checklist**. When a scope repeats the same work for several displays, each display gets its own items.
