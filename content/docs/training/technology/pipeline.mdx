## Where your deals live

Your work sits in a few saved views. Open the **view dropdown** at the top of the Opportunities list and you'll find:

- **Proposal Pipeline** — the active queue, sorted by what's due.
- **Bid Tracker** — a board view of bids by status (received → submitted → won/lost).
- **Pipeline by Business Unit** — everything rolled up by vertical, so you can isolate Technology.

**Or just ask:** *"Show me the Technology pipeline."*

## Moving a deal forward

As a deal progresses, keep its **stage** and **bid status** current — that's what keeps everyone's dashboards accurate.

1. Open the opportunity.
2. Update the **Stage** as it moves (e.g. Meeting → Proposal).
3. When it lands, set **bid status** to **Won** and fill in the close details.

**Or just ask:** *"Move the Suns scoreboard deal to won."*

<Callout title="Why this matters">
The leadership dashboards and forecasts are built straight from these fields. Keeping your stage current is the single most useful habit — it means no one has to chase you for a status update.
</Callout>

## Your dashboard tab

The **ANC 2026 Company Dashboard** has tabs by vertical. Open the **Technology** tab to see your booked revenue, margin, and forecast in one place — no spreadsheet needed.

**Or just ask:** *"How much Technology revenue have we booked this year?"*

## Now you try →

Open your **Proposal Pipeline** view, pick one deal, and move its stage forward one step. Then open the **Technology** dashboard tab.
