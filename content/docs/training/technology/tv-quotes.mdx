## Start a quote

In the Proposal Engine sidebar, under **Build & estimate**, open **TV Quotes** and select **New TV quote**. Give the quote a title and the client, and add the venue and address if you have them — they carry into the document and the purchase order.

## Add the TVs, mounts, labor and shipping

Select **+ TV** and pick the model off the list. The description and the unit cost fill in from the current LG price list; set the quantity and where the screens go. Add **Mount**, **Accessory**, **Labor**, **Shipping** or **Other** lines the same way and type their cost.

- The **Default margin %** applies to every line. Type a margin on a line to change just that line, or type a **Unit sell** price to set it outright — a typed price shows in blue, and the arrow beside it returns to the margin price.
- **Tax** marks the lines sales tax applies to; labor is untaxed by default. Enter the **Sales tax %** for the job.
- **PO** marks the lines that go on the vendor purchase order. Equipment is on by default; ANC's own labor and shipping are off.

The totals on the right follow as you type: TVs, mounts and accessories, labor and services, shipping, the tax, the total, and underneath, your cost, the margin and what the vendor PO comes to. The quote saves itself.

## Send it three ways

- **Excel workbook** downloads three sheets: the client **Quote**, the internal **Cost & Margin** sheet, and the **Purchase Order** sheet, all with live formulas.
- **Create the client document** opens a proposal in the engine with the equipment table, the introduction and the scope of work already in place. Export it as PDF or Word from the usual export step. **Refresh the client document** rewrites the table after you change the lines.
- **Issue PO** fills ANC's purchase authorization form from the equipment lines at cost. Enter the vendor, who requested it, the delivery address, when it is needed on site, the payment terms and the approver. The number is assigned once and never changes; **Update the PO** keeps it while you edit the details. **Download the purchase order PDF** is the signed-ready form.

## Link the CRM deal

Search the CRM by deal or account name in the **CRM deal** box and choose the deal. When the PO is issued, its value goes onto that deal as the **Technology Vendor PO Value**, the vendor is set, and a note lands on the deal's timeline with the order detail and a link back to the quote. Creating the client document posts a note too.
