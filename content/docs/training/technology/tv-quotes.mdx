## Start with the client and scope

Under **Build & estimate**, open **TV Quotes** and select **New TV quote**. Name the quote, then open **Client & terms**. Enter the client, venue, address, contact, quote expiry and lead time.

Choose an existing **CRM deal** to fill available client details into empty fields. Information you have already entered stays in place. Check the resulting details and select **Save client & terms**.

The quote list supports search, stage filtering and **My quotes**. Use the duplicate action in a quote to start an independent draft from an existing package.

## Choose equipment and set prices

Select **Add TV**. Search the model, filter by size or environment, and compare up to three displays. Product pictures represent the manufacturer’s series; appearance can vary by screen size. Adding a model fills in its description and maintained unit cost.

Set the quantity, then expand the line’s details to assign a location. Add **Mount**, **Accessory**, **Labor**, **Shipping** or **Other** lines as needed. Lines can be duplicated, grouped by location or removed with an undo action.

- **Default margin %** applies unless a line has its own margin. A typed **Unit sell** price overrides the margin calculation for that line.
- **Tax** selects the lines that receive sales tax. Labor is untaxed by default; set the job’s **Sales tax %**.
- **PO** selects what the vendor supplies. Display equipment is included by default; ANC labor and shipping are excluded by default.

Totals remain at the bottom while you work. Check cost, margin, vendor cost and the client total before exporting.

## Reuse a package or paste rows

Open **Packages** to save the current equipment as a reusable package. A package includes quantities, costs, margins and locations; client and purchase-order details remain with the quote.

Set **Number of packages** and an optional **Apply location**, then select **Add package**. The multiplier applies to each saved quantity. Review the resulting lines and costs for the new job.

**Paste from Excel** accepts six columns in order: **Kind**, **Model**, **Description**, **Location**, **Qty**, **Unit cost**. Select **Validate & add rows**, then check the imported lines.

## Review the client document and workbook

Open **Review & export** to inspect the client preview, quantities, scope, terms and total.

- **Internal workbook** downloads the **Quote**, **Cost & Margin** and **Purchase Order** sheets. Keep the full workbook internal because it includes costs and margins.
- **Prepare client document** creates the proposal. Use **Open PDF / Word document** to open it and export the client file. After changing the quote, **Refresh client document** updates the proposal.
- Preparing a document sets the quote to **Prepared**. It does not send the document. Mark **Sent to client** after sharing it.

## Issue and revise the purchase order

Open **Purchasing** and review the vendor, requester, department, delivery address, needed-by information, payment terms and approver. **Issue purchase order** assigns an order number and retains the equipment and delivery details issued at that point.

Later quote edits do not alter the issued PDF. **Issue new PO revision** captures the updated order under the same number. **Quote history** keeps the issued revisions available to download. The workbook’s **Purchase Order** sheet reflects the issued order; its quote and cost sheets reflect the current estimate.

With a linked deal, the issued PO value and vendor are recorded on that deal with a timeline note. **Purchasing** shows the update status. If it needs attention, use **Retry CRM update** after checking the deal link.

## Check saving before leaving

Wait for **Saved** before leaving the quote. If saving fails, the draft remains in the editor and exports wait until saving succeeds. Retry the save or download a recovery copy. If another window has changed the quote, retain your draft and load the latest version before continuing.
