## What's due this week

Your home base for proposal work is the **Estimation & Proposals** view. It shows the deals that need attention, sorted by **proposal due date**, with the columns that matter — stage, priority, pricing status.

- **Click it:** Opportunities → view dropdown → **Estimation & Proposals**.
- **Or just ask:** *"What proposals are due this week?"*

## Proposal stages

Each deal moves through proposal stages (RFP → pricing → proposal → and so on). Keep the **Proposal Stage** field current so the view stays accurate and nothing slips.

- **Click it:** open the opportunity → set **Proposal Stage**. When pricing is finished, mark **Pricing Complete**.
- **Or just ask:** *"Mark pricing complete on the Rice University deal."*

## Finding the estimate or proposal

Each opportunity gathers its related work on its own page — so the estimate, documents, and links live with the deal, not in a separate folder.

1. Open the opportunity.
2. Scroll its page to the related sections (estimates, documents, links).
3. Open what you need right there.

**Or just ask:** *"Show me the estimate on this deal."*

<Callout title="Tip">
Proposal due dates drive the whole view's sort order. Setting an accurate due date when a deal comes in is what keeps "what's due this week" actually correct.
</Callout>

Open the **Estimation & Proposals** view, pick a deal with an upcoming due date, and confirm its proposal stage is current.
