**After this lesson** you'll be able to read your Technology numbers without opening a spreadsheet — and pull any of them with a single question.


## Your tab on the dashboard

The **ANC 2026 Company Dashboard** has a tab per vertical. Open the **Technology** tab and you see your booked revenue, margin, and forecast — your side only, not the whole company's.

**Or just ask:** *"How much Technology revenue have we booked this year?"*

## Your biggest open deals

There's a **top open deals** view, and you can sort your pipeline by value. Use it to make sure the deals that matter most are getting your attention.

**Or just ask:** *"What are my biggest open opportunities right now?"*

## Comparing how you're tracking

The dashboards let you look across periods, so you can see the trend — not just today's snapshot.

**Or just ask:** *"Compare this quarter's Technology pipeline to last quarter."*

<Callout title="The fastest way to any number">
You rarely need to hunt for a report. Ask the assistant — *"how much have I won this quarter?"* or *"what's the Technology forecast for this year?"* — and it pulls the figure straight from the live data.
</Callout>
