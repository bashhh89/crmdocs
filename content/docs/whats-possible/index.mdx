# What Else Is Possible

The three tools — Proposal Engine, CRM, Service Dashboard — already share information automatically. That **pattern** is the foundation. Once a system can talk to another system, dozens of new possibilities open up that weren't on the table before.

This section walks through the most valuable next moves. Each one is a real, build-able feature — based on patterns that are already proven in production today.

## The thinking

Every upgrade in this section follows the same logic:

1. **A pattern already works.** (Example: every proposal Natalia generates auto-lands on the right deal in the CRM.)
2. **The same pattern can do more.** (Example: an inbound RFP email could auto-create the deal *before* Natalia even sees it.)
3. **The benefit is concrete.** (Example: no more "this RFP came in three days ago and nobody saw it.")

We don't pitch greenfield software. We pitch *next steps from where we already are*.

## What's in this section

- **[Proposal Engine upgrades](./proposal-engine-upgrades)** — turn the engine into a more proactive sales partner: inbound email capture, stuck-deal nudges, AI win/loss learnings
- **[Service Dashboard upgrades](./service-dashboard-upgrades)** — predictive maintenance, automatic renewal alerts, service signals that become upsells
- **[Account intelligence](./account-intelligence)** — cross-system views: a customer health score, an account-360 single pane, a Monday-morning AI briefing
- **[Slack-first ops](./slack-first-ops)** — pull the work into Slack. Ask the bot anything. Log a ticket from a hallway conversation.

## How to read each pitch

Every page below uses the same shape so you can flip through them quickly:

> **The benefit** — what this gets you
> **What we already do** — the proven pattern this builds on
> **What changes** — the new capability in plain language
> **Rough effort** — how big the lift is

The point is to make each upgrade feel **inevitable** — not "should we?" but "when?"

## A reminder: nothing here is hypothetical software

Every idea in this section uses pieces ANC already has running in production. The product catalog sync, the real-time ticket sync, the universal CRM push, the Scout AI helper, the @ANC Slack bot, the venue–company link, the per-fiscal-year revenue rollup — all of these are live. The upgrades are about **wiring them together in new ways**.
