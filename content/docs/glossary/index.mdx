## Tools and systems

**Proposal Engine** — The tool at [proposals.anc.com](https://proposals.anc.com). Pricing deals, generating proposals, building SOWs, responding to RFPs. Sales-side home.

**CRM** — The tool at [crm.ancsports.net](https://crm.ancsports.net). The system of record for every account, deal, dashboard, and report. Replaced Salesforce in April 2026.

**Service Dashboard** — The tool at [services.ancsports.net](https://services.ancsports.net). Day-to-day operations: events, tickets, technicians, venue health. Operations-side home.

**Twenty** — The CRM platform ANC's CRM is built on (open-source). When someone says "Twenty," they usually mean the CRM.

**Scout** — The AI helper inside the CRM. 30 ANC-specific skills, account-aware. Was named "Boyka" until April 2026.

**@ANC (Slack bot)** — The Slack version of Scout. Same brain, same skills. Anything you can ask Scout in the CRM, you can ask @ANC in Slack.

**ANC Assistant** — The AI helper on this docs site (docs.ancsports.net). Knows the docs deeply; suggests Scout / @ANC for live data.

**Copilot** — The slide-out AI panel inside the Proposal Engine. Helps with quick form changes ("set bond to 3%") and knowledge questions ("what's the difference between Halo and Aura?").

**ANC app vs Standard app** — Two layouts inside the CRM. **ANC** is the customized one with all the ANC dashboards and the Scout helper. **Standard** is the vanilla layout. Most users want ANC. Switch at the top-left app picker.

## The three verticals

The CRM rolls every deal up to one of three verticals. This is Jireh's mental model.

**Technology** — LED hardware, LiveSync, portables, products. Hardware-led deals.

**Venue Services** — Recurring service contracts, on-site ops. Service-led deals.

**Media / Sponsorship** — Ad sales, graphics, content, feeds. Advertising-led deals (the Hankook MLB pattern).

## Sales and proposal terms

**Deal** — Same thing as Opportunity. ANC uses "Deal" colloquially.

**Opportunity** — A deal in the CRM. About 9,994 of them as of late April 2026.

**Stage** — The high-level lifecycle: New → Screening → Meeting → Proposal → Customer.

**Bid Status** — ANC's bid flow. The Bid Tracker Kanban runs on this. Values: RFP Received, Scoping, Bid Submitted, Shortlisted, Won, Lost, No Bid.

**Proposal Stage** — The proposal-pipeline status: RFP, Sales Lead, BAFO, LOI, Existing Client Budget.

**Priority** — Top-of-list flags for the proposal team: Priority 1 RFP, Best And Final, Pricing Complete.

**Pricing Complete** — A flag set automatically when the Proposal Engine generates a one-pager. The deal is ready to send.

**Mirror Mode** — The Proposal Engine feature that produces an exact-fidelity Excel-to-PDF proposal. Natalia's golden rules apply.

**Universal CRM Push** — The behavior where every artifact generated in the Proposal Engine (PDF, SOW, Excel, one-pager) auto-attaches to the matching deal in the CRM. No manual upload.

**Universal CRM Push, the silent-failure fix** — Before April 24, 2026, push failures were silently swallowed. After: every failure logs and is replayable.

**RFP Analyzer** — The Proposal Engine feature that reads an inbound RFP PDF and extracts requirements, pricing, schedules, and drawings. Production-frozen since April 2, 2026 — no edits without explicit approval.

**Estimator** — The Proposal Engine workflow for pricing deals. Includes AI Quick / AI Reason / AI Chat helpers.

**SOW (Scope of Work)** — The document defining what ANC will install or deliver. Two flavors: **Premium SOW** (default since April 3, 2026) and **Installation SOW**.

**Submittal Compiler** — The Proposal Engine tool that rolls specs, cut sheets, and drawings into the submittal package the installer actually uses.

**Intelligence Mode** — A pricing engine inside the Proposal Engine. Built and shipped, currently hidden behind a feature flag pending billing decision.

**Currency FX** — Multi-currency support across the Proposal Engine (USD/CAD/EUR/GBP). Live since April 17, 2026.

## Operations terms

**Event** — A game, concert, or service event at a venue. About 150,197 of them in the CRM. Sourced from Ticketmaster, EventFinder, or manual add.

**Service Ticket** — An issue raised against a venue. About 3,901 of them in the CRM.

**Voicemail-to-Ticket** — When a client calls the support line and leaves a message, a ticket is auto-created within ~30 seconds with the recording, transcript, and caller number.

**Workflow** (in Service Dashboard) — The 3-step state machine for every event needing staffing: Pending → Checked-in → Game-ready → Post-game submitted.

**Walkthrough Log** — A record of a venue visit: date, time, technician, locations visited, issues found. About 15,465 of them in the CRM.

**Client Portal** — A token-gated public page per venue, where clients can see status and open tickets without an ANC login.

**SLA** — Service Level Agreement. Each ticket has a first-response and resolution deadline based on priority. Critical = 1h response / 4h resolution. Low = 8h / 72h.

**Real-time CRM Sync** — As of April 20, 2026, every ticket / event / workflow change syncs from Service Dashboard to CRM in about a second.

| **Revenue Splits** | 5,160 | Per-fiscal-year deal breakdowns |

**Two hubs**: every record traces back to a **Company** (commercial side) or a **Venue** (physical side). Most records link to both.

**Scaffolded objects** — schemas built but not yet populated:
- **Services** — recurring service contracts (Monthly Value, Service Type, Start/End Date)
- **Estimates / Estimate Lines** — wired for Scout's Quick Estimate skill
- **RFP Analyses** — schema ready (23 fields, links to Account/Deal/Venue), awaiting the Proposal Engine bridge
- **Workflows** — the CRM's native automation engine, unused so far

## Key fields you'll see

**`pricingComplete`** — Boolean. True when the Proposal Engine has generated a one-pager for the deal.

**`pricingCompleteDate`** — Date stamp set when `pricingComplete` flips to true.

**`bidStatus`** — Where the deal is in the bid flow. See "Bid Status" above.

**`proposalDueDate`** — When the proposal is due to the client.

**`substantialCompletionDate`** — When the work was substantially complete on a won deal.

**`paidAmount` / `percentPaid`** — Finance status on a deal.

**`totalProjectRevenue` / `totalProjectMargin`** — What Salesforce historically said about the deal. Mirror fields kept for cross-reference.

**`amount` (Total Deal Revenue)** — Auto-rollup of revenue2026 + revenue2027 + ... + revenue2030.

**`margin` (Total Deal Margin)** — Auto-rollup of margin2026 through margin2030.

**`league`** — NFL / NBA / MLB / NHL / NCAA / MLS / WNBA / NWSL etc. Inherited from Company.

**`businessUnit`** — TECHNOLOGY / VENUE_SERVICES / MEDIA_SPONSORSHIP. The three-vertical roll-up.

**`assignedEstimatorName`** — Who's pricing the deal.

**`proposalOwner`** — Natalia's role on the deal (the second assigned estimator).

**`Services Dashboard ID`** — The bridge field on Service Tickets, Staff Members, and Venues that links a CRM record to its Service Dashboard counterpart.

## AI helpers and automation

**Scout's 30 skills** — The CRM-side AI's specialized capabilities. Examples:

- `pipeline-tracker` — open deals at risk
- `contract-tracker` — backlog + payment status
- `rfp-analyzer` — parse RFP documents
- `quick-estimator` — back-of-napkin pricing
- `sow-generator` — SOW + one-pager
- `designer-ai` — generate venue mockup images (Gemini Nano Banana Pro 2)
- `ticket-triage` — categorize new tickets
- `venue-health-report` — per-venue rollup
- `failure-pattern-detective` — recurring ticket patterns
- `inventory-locator` — find parts/assets
- `event-staffing-assistant` — staff an event
- `performance-analytics` — team / league breakdowns
- `ops-daily-digest` — daily summary
- `product-catalog` — LED product spec lookup
- (...and 16 more)

The full list lives on the [Scout Skills page](/docs/user-guide/capabilities/scout-skills) with example prompts.

**Designer AI** — Inside the CRM, ask Scout for a graphic and it generates one in ~20 seconds via Gemini 3 Pro Image Preview, attaching the result to a new Design Request.

**LED Diagnostic** — Inside the Service Dashboard, snap a photo of a malfunctioning LED, get a structured diagnosis (issue type, likely cause, suggested fix, urgency).

**Workflows (CRM-native automation)** — The CRM has an unused automation engine. Many of the upgrades in the [What Else Is Possible](/docs/whats-possible) section can be configured here without code.
