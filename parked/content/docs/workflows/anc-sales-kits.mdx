## Create the presentation

Open **Content library → Sales kits**. Enter the **Client name**, **Venue name**, **Meeting goal**, and **Presented by**. The preview reflects your client details.

Click a section title to preview it. Use its checkbox to include or omit it. Keep the opening, the closing, and at least one additional section. Sections remain in the prepared order. **Talk track for this section** provides a short speaking cue.

The kit includes ANC technology, design, content control, venue operations, ANC Studios creative, and Toyota Center and M&T Bank Stadium references. Reference venues keep their real names when you personalize the presentation.

Choose **Create client presentation**. When **Presentation saved** appears, open the presentation to review and edit your copy. Use the existing **Share** control only when the client version is ready. Creation itself saves a draft; it does not publish a client link.

## Use matching venue creative

Under **Matching venue creative**, play the ANC Studios reel or download the reel and transparent ANC logo. You can upload these files to venue screens in the Venue Showroom. This is a manual download and upload; the two workspaces do not automatically synchronize.

The reel is an ANC creative reference, not footage of the prospective client's venue. The project examples describe their named reference venues, not a promised scope or price for the new client.

## Prepare with the sales brief

Choose **Open sales brief** after creation. You can also reopen it from the presentation builder's **More → Internal sales brief** menu.

The brief includes the meeting goal, presenter, kit version, and talking points in presentation order. It is available inside the signed-in workspace and is excluded from the client presentation link and presentation PDF. Keep internal preparation notes out of the client-facing slides.

If you add a new section to the client copy, prepare its talk track separately; the brief flags sections that have no copied note.

## Maintain the kit

The creative team uses **Review kit source → Edit kit master**. Edit and save the master in the presentation builder, then return and choose **Reload source**.

Review the sections and their internal talk tracks, then choose **Publish kit revision**. Every section needs a talk track. The supported personalization fields are `{{clientName}}`, `{{venueName}}`, `{{meetingGoal}}`, and `{{presenterName}}`.

A revision supplies future client copies. Existing presentations and their copied talk tracks stay unchanged. If someone changes the source while you are reviewing it, reload and review the current material before publishing.

Designers maintain the standard, sales personalizes the conversation, and Natalia assembles from prepared material. These responsibilities use the platform's existing shared workspace access; they are not separate permission roles or an automatic approval process.
