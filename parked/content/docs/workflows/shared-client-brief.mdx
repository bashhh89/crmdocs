## Start with the meeting

Open **Client briefs → New client brief**. Enter the client, venue, audience and meeting goal. Set the deadline and choose the brief owner. **Save brief** keeps one shared revision for everyone.

Under **Opportunity**, search by account or venue and select the real CRM opportunity. The CRM remains the opportunity record; the brief connects the work around it. **Copy internal link** gives colleagues the brief address. They must sign in to read it.

You can also open **More → Shared client briefs** from a presentation to find its linked briefs or start a new one with that presentation already connected.

## Connect the material

Choose an existing presentation, or select a sales kit and choose **Create from this brief**. Save the client, venue, audience and meeting goal first. The new presentation uses the saved client details and goal. Review its sections in the presentation builder and use **Share → Go live, create link and copy** to publish the saved client version.

Add the showroom's **published client link**, ending in `/show/…`. In the showroom, save the project, publish it, and copy that client link. A workspace access link is not a published project link.

Select the supporting assets from the existing library. Assign a person to each deliverable, add production notes, and mark each completed deliverable **Ready**. These readiness checks are the team's assessment; they do not replace creative review.

## Set named responsibilities

The owner and workspace administrators manage the brief's permissions:

- **Brief editors** can prepare the brief and send it to Review.
- **Handoff approvers** can approve it for sales and record sharing.
- The **owner and workspace administrators** can do both.
- Other active workspace members can read the brief.

These permissions govern the **brief and handoff**. The CRM, showroom and presentation keep their existing source editing and access settings. Assigning a deliverable does not itself grant editing permission; add the person as a brief editor if they need to update it.

## Move the handoff forward

**Preparing → Review → Ready for sales → Shared**

**Send to review** becomes available when the meeting details, deadline, opportunity, presentation, showroom, assets and assigned deliverables are complete. Saving changes during Review returns the brief to Preparing.

A named approver chooses **Approve for sales**. The platform checks the published presentation against its saved draft and checks the showroom's published material. It records the approved versions and prepares the client presentation link. Missing, unpublished or changed material must be resolved before approval.

Choose **Copy approved client links** to copy the presentation and showroom links together. The brief rechecks readiness before copying. Internal notes and permissions remain inside the workspace.

After sending the client links yourself, enter **Shared with** and choose **Record as shared**. This records the recipient and time; it does not send a message.

## Keep the revision clear

The brief shows its current revision, approval identity and presentation version. **Revision history** lets you inspect earlier saved context and handoff decisions.

Approved briefs are read-only until an approver or owner chooses **Reopen for preparation**. Review the updated material again before approving. Reopening preserves the history and does not revoke links already distributed from the source applications.

If the linked presentation or showroom changes, or the approved presentation link is revoked, the brief flags that its approved material needs attention. If another person saves first, reload and review the current revision before trying your changes again.
